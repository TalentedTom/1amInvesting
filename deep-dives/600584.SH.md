## Supercycle: AI Advanced Packaging + China Semiconductor Localization

## Core Thesis: China's Advanced-Packaging Capacity Platform

长电科技 is China's #1 OSAT and global #3 (12.7% global, >35% domestic), with global manufacturing across China, Korea, and Singapore. The thesis is NOT "generic OSAT." It is: if China wants domestic AI chips, domestic memory, domestic automotive chips, and domestic HPC modules, it needs high-end packaging capacity. JCET is one of the few Chinese companies with scale, global customers, and advanced-packaging process depth.

**Advanced packaging capabilities (2025 annual report confirmed):**
- 2.5D high-density multi-dimensional heterogeneous integration: **entered mass production**
- XDFOI (chiplet Fan-Out Integration): production-ready
- CPO / silicon photonics engine: **samples delivered and verified**
- Memory packaging: DRAM and Flash, cooperating with world's top three memory manufacturers
- FCBGA, SiP, WLP, flip-chip, automotive, power, RF
- HBM packaging yield 98.5%, ~20% global market share (per Chinese analysis)

**2025 results:** Revenue ¥388.71亿 (+8.1%, record high). Advanced packaging revenue ¥270亿 (record, ~70% of total). Computing electronics +42.6% YoY, industrial/medical +40.6%, automotive +31.7%. R&D ¥20.86亿 (+21.4%). OCF ¥46.52亿. NI ¥15.65亿 (margin thin at 4.0% due to new factory ramp + raw material inflation + FX losses).

**Q1 2026:** Revenue ¥91.71亿, NI ¥2.90亿 (+42.7% YoY), OCF ¥17.79亿 (+55.4%). Computing electronics +14.2% continuing momentum.

**The capex commitment:** 2026 fixed-asset budget ~¥100亿. NEW: ¥78亿 Shanghai Lingang high-end advanced packaging factory (two phases, Phase 1 completion ~2H 2028). This is China betting on domestic advanced packaging at scale.

**Critical distinction from materials names:** JCET does NOT capture customer capex the way NAURA captures equipment orders. JCET SPENDS capex to build capacity, then earns revenue by running that capacity. The demand-side model is: AI/GPU/HBM/DRAM chip demand → advanced package demand → JCET utilization + mix improvement → revenue and margin expansion.

**Rule 6/9 result:** FAIL global chokepoint (ASE, Amkor, TSMC packaging exist). PASS China domestic packaging bottleneck. PASS AI infrastructure derivative. Classification: 📊 BENEFICIARY — benefits from the supercycle, doesn't control a physics layer.
