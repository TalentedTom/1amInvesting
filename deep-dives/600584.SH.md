## Verdict

The revised base sits between the first conservative RMB 58 estimate and the prior workbook's RMB 75 base. The business is likely under-modeled if it is treated as a mature commodity OSAT, but the evidence does not support carrying the old workbook's rapid margin ramp or a global-chokepoint multiple.

The balanced base therefore prices the breadcrumbs without treating them as confirmed revenue:

- H1 preliminary profit indicates a meaningful Q2 step-up, but the range midpoint is a model anchor—not a reported result.
- China's packaging TAM and JCET's broad advanced-packaging exposure support share and mix gains.
- Lingang creates a credible 2029–2030 capacity option, but Phase 1 is not scheduled to complete until H2 2028 and is not included in the 2026–2027 base.
- FY2029–2030 ceilings are intentionally withheld pending Lingang qualification, utilization, and customer-conversion evidence. They are not missing values.

## Why the prior model needed revision

The former quarterly model showed Q1 2026 operating profit of RMB 1.04B. That figure appears to have been gross profit rather than statutory operating profit. The statutory Q1 operating-profit anchor is approximately **RMB 295M**, producing a **3.22% operating margin** on RMB 9.171B of revenue.

The prior model then rose to RMB 73.4B of FY2028 revenue and a 14.6% Q4 operating margin. That path was too aggressive relative to current utilization, the timing of Lingang, and comparable OSAT economics. The corrected base reaches RMB 62.0B of FY2028 revenue and a 9.6% full-year operating margin.

## Business and TAM identity

JCET should be modeled as a packaging-volume and complexity beneficiary, not by multiplying wafer-fab-equipment spending. The closest listed operating analogy is ASE: the relevant revenue bridge is packaged units × package complexity × utilization × blended ASP/mix.

Key anchors:

- 2025 China OSAT TAM: approximately **RMB 333.2B**.
- 2026 industry growth anchor: approximately **15.3%**.
- 2027 normalized growth range used for the cross-check: **8.5%–12%**.
- JCET's reported 2025 advanced-packaging-related revenue: approximately **RMB 27B**, but this is a broad category and should not be treated as narrow 2.5D/3D revenue.
- FY2027 base revenue of RMB 53.0B implies only modest share gain, approximately **12.3%–12.7%** of the relevant China OSAT market under the normalized TAM range.
- Lingang project investment: approximately **RMB 7.8B**, with Phase 1 completion expected in H2 2028.

## Scenario framing

- **Base: RMB 67.** FY2028 revenue RMB 62B and operating margin 9.6%; no Lingang contribution is pulled forward.
- **Evidence-upside zone: RMB 75–86.** Requires visible 2.5D/XDFOI conversion, sustained high utilization, and FY2028 operating margin around 10.5%–11%.
- **RMB 96+ bull case.** Requires economics close to RMB 73B revenue and a 12% operating margin, or a valuation multiple above 20x. This is not the current base.

## Breadcrumbs that can move the base higher

1. H1 2026 report confirms profit quality, statutory operating leverage, and cash conversion.
2. Q3 attributable net profit reaches or exceeds RMB 500M without working-capital stress.
3. XDFOI/2.5D disclosures move from capability and customer sampling to measurable volume revenue.
4. Lingang construction, tool move-in, qualification, and customer commitments remain on or ahead of schedule.
5. CPO-related packaging progresses from samples to high-volume production.

## Risks and disconfirming evidence

- Broad “advanced packaging” revenue can overstate exposure to the narrow AI accelerator opportunity.
- Customer concentration and pricing pressure can prevent TAM growth from translating into margin expansion.
- High capex can raise accounting profit while suppressing free cash flow.
- Domestic and international OSAT competition can cap share gains.
- Geopolitical and export restrictions can affect equipment access and customer mix.
- A delayed Lingang ramp would remove the most important post-2028 capacity option.
