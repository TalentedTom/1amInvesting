## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

JCET converts wafer output into reliable packaged chips and tested systems. Higher package complexity, power density, memory content and local sourcing expand its opportunity. XDFOI, wafer-level packaging and multi-die integration strengthen customer relationships, but ASE, Amkor, other Chinese OSATs and foundry packaging remain alternatives. Manufacturing scale and qualification create value; this is not a global sole-source choke point.

Chip design and wafer fabrication → bumping, assembly, advanced packaging and test → system OEM. JCET earns processing and integration revenue, not the entire chip selling price. Broad advanced-packaging revenue is not synonymous with narrow2.5D/3D AI accelerator packaging. Memory price inflation alone does not increase package units; volume and complexity are the relevant bridge.
