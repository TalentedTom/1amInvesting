## Supercycle: AI Passive Components (MLCC + Chip Resistors)

## Core Thesis: China's Murata

MLCCs are the rice of electronics — an AI server uses ~30,000 (30x a smartphone), a GB200 NVL72 cabinet ~600,000, the VR300 ~330,000 passives. Fenghua is China's **only full-spectrum passive-component manufacturer** (MLCC + resistors + inductors + capacitors + varistors + thermistors), national champion (单项冠军) in both MLCC and chip resistors. It is not competing to be Murata globally — it IS Murata for the Chinese domestic ecosystem, where the substitution mandate (国产替代) plus trade restrictions make Huawei, ZTE, and BYD structurally unable to rely on Japanese/Korean suppliers for strategic products.

**The demand state (May-Jun 2026):** order intake **suspended** on all 0402/0603 resistor and MLCC lines — demand exceeds capacity at ~90% utilization — with two price rounds in five months (effective Apr 1, Jul 1). Industry-wide confirmation: Murata and SEMCO >90% utilization; high-end MLCC structurally tight for **1-2 years with 20+ week lead times and CSPs signing LTAs** (Jun 2026 channel); the squeeze widens when Vera Rubin absorbs high-end capacity in H2.
