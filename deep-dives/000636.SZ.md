## Supercycle: AI Passive Components (MLCC + Chip Resistors for AI Datacenter)

## Core Thesis: China's Murata — National Champion in the "Rice of Electronics"

MLCCs are the most consumed passive component in electronics — every circuit board needs them for filtering, coupling, and energy storage. A single AI server uses 30,000 MLCCs (30x a smartphone). An NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 cabinet uses 600,000 MLCCs. As AI infrastructure scales, MLCC demand is growing exponentially.

Fenghua is China's only full-spectrum passive component manufacturer: MLCC + chip resistors + inductors + capacitors + varistors + thermistors. National champion (单项冠军) designation in both MLCC and chip resistors. Domestic supply chain security for Huawei, ZTE, BYD — these customers cannot rely on Japanese/Korean suppliers (Murata, Samsung Electro-Mechanics) given trade tensions.

## Key Monitoring Question

Will Fenghua management hike prices aggressively, or will national champion pressure keep prices lower for domestic customers (Huawei, ZTE, BYD)?

Every other supply-constrained company in our portfolio saw margin expansion when demand exceeded supply. But Fenghua operates within China's industrial policy — there may be implicit pressure to keep prices affordable for strategic domestic customers, especially during trade tensions when these OEMs have no alternative suppliers. If management prioritizes national mission over margin maximization, OP stays 15-20% instead of 25-30%.

Monitor: quarterly GM and OP trends. If GM stays below 22% despite order suspension and dual price hikes, the national champion pressure thesis is confirmed and margins should be modeled lower. If GM crosses 25%+, the company is pricing like a true supply-constrained monopoly and our 20-30% OP trajectory is correct.
