## Core Thesis: Smartphone SoC Company → Google TPU Platform Supplier

MediaTek is no longer just a smartphone SoC company. It is becoming Google's primary custom AI ASIC partner across TPU v8/v9/v10. Q1 2026 call: first AI ASIC project to contribute ~US$2B revenue in Q4 2026 (2x prior expectation). Second project targets mass production by late 2027. Jeff Pu/local supply chain: Triggerfish (v9 variant) at ~$18K ASP, 50% of v9 mix, exclusive MTK. Icefish (v10) also MTK. Broadcom's Bladerunner "hasn't progressed as a project."

**FY2025:** Revenue NT$596.0B (+12.3%), GM 47.5%, OP margin 17.4%, EPS NT$66.16.
**Q1 2026:** Rev NT$149.2B (-2.7% YoY). Mobile 49% (-17% QoQ), Smart Edge 46% (+23% QoQ). AI ASIC not yet in revenue.

**The transformation math:** Cloud AI ASIC from NT$10B (2025) → NT$63B (2026, Q4 only) → NT$450B (2027) → NT$1,000B (2028). If Morgan Stanley is right that Google TPU reaches 63% of MTK revenue by 2028, this is the largest SoC company transformation in semiconductor history.

**Rule 6/9:** FAIL chokepoint (Google can multi-source: Broadcom, Marvell, internal). PASS Google TPU platform beneficiary — increasingly important to v8/v9/v10 roadmap. ⚡ BOTTLENECK.
