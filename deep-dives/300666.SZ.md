## Supercycle: China Semiconductor Materials/Components (国产替代) + Memory Structural (DB6)

## Core Thesis: The Chip's Grain + ESC Second Engine

Sputtering targets are "芯片的粮食" — every chip manufactured via PVD requires target material sputtered onto wafers to form metal interconnects, barrier layers, and storage structures. Targets are consumed during use, creating RECURRING consumable demand proportional to wafer throughput. KFMI is global #2 in semiconductor sputtering targets (~26% share by 2025), qualified at TSMC 3nm, SMIC, SK Hynix, UMC, and all major global fabs. Cu-Mn targets (previously Japanese-monopolized) are the key breakthrough for advanced logic interconnects. Founder 姚力军 (ex-Honeywell) built full vertical integration from ultra-high-purity metal refining through target manufacturing over 20 years.

**Capex-to-materials annuity:** NAURA captures equipment capex first. KFMI captures the recurring target/parts replacement after wafer starts rise. CXMT/YMTC capex → installed capacity → higher wafer starts → recurring target consumption + parts replacement + ESC demand. The lag makes KFMI less front-loaded than NAURA but potentially more recurring.

**Advanced memory angle:** 300mm silicon targets for advanced memory in batch supply. Ultra-high-purity W and Cu-Mn targets applied to advanced memory manufacturing. Advanced DRAM, HBM, 3D NAND increase process complexity — more steps, more thin films, more stringent purity = more target consumption per wafer.

**Second growth engine — ESC (electrostatic chuck):** The swing factor. 定增 project: 5,100-unit ESC line with disclosed economics of ¥11.91亿 annual revenue, ¥4.42亿 annual NI, and **65.71% gross margin**. ESC localization <10%. Global ESC market $24.24B by 2030 (QYResearch). ESC is a high-reliability semiconductor component requiring tool/customer qualification — not instant, but the economics are transformational if proven.

**Korea base:** Korean target plant under construction for Samsung/SK Hynix proximity supply — thesis extends beyond pure China localization into global memory customer penetration.

**Rule 6/Rule 9 result:** PASS China domestic bottleneck (without KFMI, China's localization chain is meaningfully weaker). PARTIAL PASS recurring memory-capex derivative. FAIL global AI infrastructure chokepoint (JX Nippon, Honeywell, Tosoh supply the rest of the world). Classification: ⚡ BOTTLENECK, conditional on China localization regime.
