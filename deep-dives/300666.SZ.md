## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Ultrahigh-purity targets combine difficult metallurgy, customer process qualification and recurring replacement demand. More complex semiconductor manufacturing increases deposition content, while domestic tool production broadens the precision-component opportunity. These strengths support customer retention, but JX, Honeywell, Tosoh and other qualified suppliers prevent a global-monopoly classification.

Refined metals and ceramics → qualified targets/chambers/chucks → equipment makers and wafer fabs → logic, memory and other chips. Target replacement responds to process use, while equipment parts respond to new-tool orders plus servicing. A large fab budget does not become an equally large materials order.
