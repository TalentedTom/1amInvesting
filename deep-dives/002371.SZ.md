## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

NAURA provides a broad set of qualified process tools to Chinese fabs. More complex logic, DRAM, NAND and packaging increase process intensity, while domestic procurement adds potential share. Installed tools, service capability and repeated qualification support a durable franchise. Export restrictions can increase local demand while simultaneously limiting access to critical subsystems; policy does not make every NAURA tool the only technically viable option.

Fab capex → eligible process tools → customer qualification and tool delivery → acceptance → recurring service and upgrades. NAURA spans etch, deposition, thermal, wet processes, implantation, track and bonding. It does not manufacture the customer’s chips and does not capture lithography spending merely because it supplies nearby process steps.
