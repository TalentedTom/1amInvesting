## Supercycle: China Semiconductor Equipment Capex (CXMT ¥1,000亿 + YMTC + SMIC) + 国产替代

## Core Thesis: China's Applied Materials

北方华创 is China's #1 semiconductor equipment company and #7 globally (SEMI, 2024). It covers ALL front-end WFE except lithography: etch (ICP/CCP), thin film deposition (PVD/CVD/ALD), furnaces, wet clean, ion implantation (new Sirius MC 313), and track equipment (via 芯源微 acquisition). Export controls have turned NAURA from "backup option" to "only option" for Chinese fabs — "备胎转正" (backup becoming primary) is irreversible.

**Q1 2026:** Revenue ¥103.23亿 (+25.8% YoY). NI ¥16.35亿 (+3.42%, depressed by R&D +36.64%). GM 40.77%. R&D ¥14.02亿 (13.59% of revenue). OCF ¥7.48亿 (turned positive). Contract liabilities ¥42.03亿. Inventory ¥286.03亿.

**FY2025:** Revenue ¥393.53亿 (+31%). NI ¥55.22亿 (-1.8%, R&D up 47% to ¥72.77亿). GM 44.5%. Dividend ¥55亿 (nearly entire NI). "Research deep squat before profit leap."

**Revenue by product:** Etch 35% (48%+ GM, #1 profit driver). Thin film 30% (46%+ GM, #2). Together 65% revenue / 70% profit. Track equipment (芯源微). Ion implantation (new). SiC/GaN epitaxy (third-gen).

**Key customers:** SMIC (中芯国际), YMTC (长江存储), CXMT (长鑫存储). Orders through 2027. Top 2 customers >50% of revenue. Customer concentration risk is real.

**The CXMT capex connection:** CXMT Q1 2026 NI ¥248亿. FY2026E NI ¥1,000亿+. Nearly all profit → capex. CXMT + YMTC adding 10-12万片/month capacity in 2026-2027, equipment investment $15.5-18B. NAURA is primary domestic etch + thin film supplier for both. The ¥1,000亿 capex cycle is the DEMAND DRIVER.

## Rule 6 / Rule 9 Test

"If NAURA disappeared?" Short term: Chinese fabs try to buy from AMAT/Lam/TEL — but export controls BLOCK it. Medium term: AMEC/拓荆 partially fill some segments but can't cover the full platform. NAURA's PLATFORM coverage (etch + PVD + CVD + ALD + furnace + clean + implant + track) is unique in China. No other domestic company covers all front-end equipment.

Rule 6: **CONDITIONAL PASS** — NAURA is a chokepoint ONLY BECAUSE of export controls. If controls were lifted (extremely unlikely), Chinese fabs would revert to AMAT/Lam. The moat is policy-created, not physics-created. But the policy is "irreversible" under current geopolitics.

Rule 9: The edge is NOT that NAURA is better than AMAT. It's that AMAT can't sell to China. This is a REGULATORY moat, not a TECHNOLOGY moat. Higher durability than a pricing cycle (GigaDevice) but lower durability than a physics monopoly (SIVE).

## Key Risks

1. **US subsystem export controls (-1):** MATCH Act or equivalent could restrict RF power sources, high-end vacuum pumps, precision optics that go INSIDE NAURA's tools. If the subsystem supply is cut, NAURA can't build the equipment even with domestic final assembly. This is the single biggest risk — attack the components inside the tool, not the tool itself.

2. **Domestic margin competition (-1):** AMEC (688012), 拓荆 (688072), 盛美 (ACMR) all expanding. Equipment bidding is already showing "slight price declines" per analyst reports. As more domestic competitors qualify, NAURA's pricing power erodes. GM may compress from 44% toward 38-40%.

3. **Cash flow vs profit quality (-1):** Inventory ¥286亿 (73% of FY2025 revenue). Receivables growing faster than revenue. FY2025 H1 OCF was NEGATIVE ¥31.9亿. "Paper profits not converting to cash" — the equipment cycle has long delivery times (12-18 months) that create massive working capital requirements. If any major customer delays acceptance, cash flow deteriorates further.
