## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics/CPO + Custom ASIC

## Core Thesis: The Complete AI Infrastructure Stack — ASIC + Networking + Photonics + Software

Broadcom is the only company on Earth that designs custom AI accelerators (Google TPU, Meta, Anthropic), makes the networking switches (Tomahawk/Jericho), owns the photonics stack (former Bell Labs InP fab + EML lasers + DSP), AND runs a $30B software business (VMware). The result: 60% of AI server custom ASIC design partner share (Counterpoint), $73B AI order backlog, and CEO Hock Tan's "line of sight to AI chip revenue >$100B by FY2027."

The photonics angle is what brought AVGO into our framework. The Macro_Lin post identified that AVGO owns the former Bell Labs InP fab — the most storied InP facility in history — making its own EML lasers in-house. Combined with DSP and switch ASIC, this is "the most complete photonics chip stack in the industry." But the photonics business is buried inside the networking division with no separate disclosure, making it impossible to value independently.

## Position Type: 🔒 CHOKEPOINT Duopoly: Custom AI ASIC

Only AVGO + MRVL design hyperscaler custom AI chips (XPUs). 2-3yr design cycles = structural lock-in. Customers: Google (TPU), Meta, Anthropic, OpenAI. $73B AI backlog provides multi-year revenue visibility. NVIDIA invested $2B.

## The Photonics Stack (Why AVGO Is Here)

AVGO owns the most vertically integrated photonics stack in the industry:
- **InP Fab:** Former Bell Labs facility. Makes EML lasers in-house.
- **EML Lasers:** First-tier 200G EML producer. One of only 3-4 companies globally (alongside LITE, COHR, Sourcelight).
- **DSP:** PAM4 DSPs for 800G/1.6T transceivers
- **Switch ASIC:** Tomahawk 5 (51.2Tb/s), Jericho3-AI
- **CPO:** Building co-packaged optics on own platform
- **200G SerDes:** Industry leadership for 1.6T transition

This is buried inside "AI Networking" with no separate revenue disclosure. The total addressable market for photonics within AVGO is impossible to isolate from public filings.
