## Thesis

FOCI is TSMC's primary FAU (Fiber Array Unit) partner for COUPE CPO packaging. Appears FIVE times in Goldman Sachs' CPO supply chain map — FAU, couplers/splitters, filters, attenuators, WDM — more than any other single company. TSMC handles SiPho chip fabrication; FOCI handles the "most difficult physical connection point" — aligning optical fibers to SiPh chips at nanometer precision.

Serenity: "Made it one of my larger positions. $3.2B at the center of it all seems very promising."

TSMC COUPE advanced packaging director: "FAU would be a pretty big bottleneck and would scale up soon."

## Moat Assessment

TSMC Partnership: FOCI co-develops COUPE with TSMC. Direct CoWoS line integration. This is a qualification moat, not physics.

Geopolitical Moat: Chinese competitor TFC Optical (天孚通信) has FAU scale and yield advantage, BUT US hyperscalers and NVIDIA won't use China-based suppliers for CPO. Taiwanese analysts: "This gives FOCI an irreplaceable strategic position."

Competition: Browave (3163.TWO) attempting to enter FAU. "But FOCI's structural barriers in FAU design are built. Second source competitive threat is relatively limited."

FAU Cost Significance: Optical connection components (including FAU) = 30%+ of total CPO switch cost. This is NOT a minor component.

## Key Financials

Legacy business DECLINING: Jan-Apr 2026 revenue -17.15% YoY. Traditional fiber patch cords shrinking. CPO has NOT yet contributed revenue. This is pre-revenue for the primary thesis — same pattern as SOI.PA (mobile declining, photonics about to inflect).

2026 CapEx: NT$1.58B (5-8x normal). NT$1B+ for CPO FAU automation and test equipment. "Extremely rare in optical component industry. Reflects extremely high order visibility."

Company changed IR email to ir@foci-cpo.com — signaling strategic pivot.
