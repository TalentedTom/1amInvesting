## Core Thesis

FOCI is TSMC's primary FAU (Fiber Array Unit) partner for COUPE CPO packaging. Appears FIVE times in Goldman Sachs' CPO supply chain map — FAU, couplers/splitters, filters, attenuators, WDM — more than any other single company. FAU = "the most difficult physical connection point" — aligning optical fibers to SiPh chips at nanometer precision. FAU + optical connection components = 30%+ of total CPO switch cost. Not a minor component.

### 2026-06-30 Update — Corning Glass Bridge / IOX Architecture Risk
Corning's Glass Bridge / IOX waveguide architecture introduces a primary architecture-displacement risk to the FOCI thesis. The risk is not that glass is bad for CPO. The risk is narrower and more important: if major CPO paths move from discrete FAU alignment toward glass-integrated waveguides, value can shift away from traditional FAU assembly and toward glass-waveguide / glass-processing suppliers.

This risk is now embedded directly in the FY2028 ceiling through a probability-weighted scenario model rather than handled as a large Base-score haircut. Base remains 49/60 because the thesis quality is still high, but the clean no-bypass FY2028 ceiling is reduced by architecture risk.

Current treatment: **low-to-medium probability, high-severity risk**. No ceiling cut beyond probability weighting unless there is named evidence that a major TSMC / NVIDIA / Broadcom / hyperscaler CPO path reduces discrete FAU content or excludes FOCI from glass-based optical assembly.
