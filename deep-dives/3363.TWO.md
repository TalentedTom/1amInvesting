## Core Thesis

FOCI is a high-beta CPO physical-interface bet. The thesis is that FOCI's fiber-array-unit (FAU) and passive optical component capabilities sit at the hardest physical attachment point between fiber and silicon-photonics optical engines.

The prior v4.3 thesis remains intact:

- FOCI is tied to the TSMC COUPE / silicon-photonics CPO ecosystem.
- FAU alignment is one of the hardest manufacturing steps because coupling fiber into optical waveguides requires extremely tight alignment.
- FOCI appears in multiple CPO passive/FAU categories in the prior supply-chain mapping.
- The company is still pre-CPO revenue; the entire stock is being valued on 2027–2028 conversion rather than current earnings.

### GlassBridge / FAU-Bypass Risk

The v4.3 artifact embedded Corning GlassBridge / IOX as an architecture-displacement risk. That treatment remains, but no further haircut is applied today.

Recent Morgan Stanley / CPO-chain commentary suggests GlassBridge is still far from mainstream mass production and currently more relevant to lower-end edge-coupling / one-dimensional layouts, while TSMC COUPE and mainstream near-term projects remain primarily grating-coupled. That weakens the immediate FAU-bypass bear case but does not eliminate the left-tail risk.

**Current treatment:** keep the existing architecture-risk probability factor until named evidence shows either:

1. a major TSMC / NVIDIA / Broadcom / hyperscaler path reduces discrete FAU content, or
2. FOCI is explicitly included in a glass-based assembly/coupling path.
