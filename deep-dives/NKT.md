## Supercycle: DB2 Offshore Wind / Energy Transmission (HVDC Submarine Cables)

## Core Thesis: The Pipeline Under the Sea — 30% HVDC Share with 9x Revenue Backlog

Only THREE companies on Earth can manufacture and install HVDC submarine power cables at scale: NKT, Prysmian, and Nexans. NKT holds ~30% market share and is building the world's largest HVDC cable production site (Karlskrona, Sweden) plus a new cable-laying vessel with record capacity. Order backlog: €10.8B+ (9x annual revenue). 75% of orders from European TSOs (government-backed entities). €900M EBITDA target by 2030.

**This is the portfolio's primary diversifier — completely uncorrelated to the photonics/AI thesis.**

## Why HVDC Cables Are a Physical Bottleneck

- Offshore wind farms generate power at sea. It must travel to shore via submarine cables.
- HVDC (high-voltage direct current) is the ONLY technology for long-distance submarine power transmission (100+ km) without unacceptable losses.
- Manufacturing HVDC cables requires specialized extrusion towers (200m+), controlled environments, and decades of process know-how.
- Installing them requires specialized cable-laying vessels that cost €300M+ each.
- **Building a new HVDC cable factory from scratch takes 5-7 years and €1B+.**
- Only 3 companies globally have the full stack: manufacturing + installation + commissioning.
