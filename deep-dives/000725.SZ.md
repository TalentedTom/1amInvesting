## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

BOE is a large display producer with related manufacturing and glass-processing capabilities. OLED penetration and declining legacy depreciation can improve display economics. Glass-core substrates provide a distinct emerging opportunity, but pilot qualification is not proof of high-volume orders or supply-chain exclusivity. This new artifact uses the existing workbook only as the before baseline; the unavailable original narrative has not been reconstructed or assumed.

Display devices: terminal demand → screen area, technology and product mix → LCD/OLED fabrication, module assembly and customer qualification. Emerging packaging: large compute packages → glass-core substrate specifications → TGV, copper fill, redistribution and multilayer build-up → qualified substrate sales. BOE can reuse glass-processing and manufacturing capabilities, but this does not establish an irreplaceable AI chokepoint. Glass core, glass interposer and temporary carrier are different products.

## 8. Multiple, shares and recognition convention

20×core OP preserves the supplied portfolio convention for a reviewable comparison. It is a judgment applied to consolidated manufacturing economics, not a conventional P/E. A mature display business with minority-owned plants does not automatically earn a pure AI-substrate multiple. The small early glass contribution receives no separate multiple uplift.

37,044.328064M issued A+B shares are used, including restricted shares already issued. Repurchased shares are not presumed cancelled; additional options are not fully reconstructed, so the dilution sensitivity remains relevant. Management’s statement that it has no current equity-issue plan is not a promise about every future expansion. Consolidated core OP includes non-controlling interests, while the denominator is listed-company shares. Without a project-by-project minority and debt bridge, the result is a framework ceiling with a material attribution limitation, not a standard equity fair value.

The valuation denominator is the ceiling quarter’s diluted-share proxy. It is not a historical EPS weighted-average assumption disguised as a fully known future capitalization. Unit factor: 1; this converts the model units to CNY per share. No cash balance, enterprise-value adjustment or after-tax earnings substitution is added to the operating-profit ceiling.
