## Supercycle: Memory Upcycle + China Localization (DB10 adjacency via niche DRAM)

## Core Thesis: Fabless Niche-Memory + MCU Platform Riding the Biggest Pricing Ramp in Decades

兆易创新 is China's only fabless IC designer ranked top-10 globally across THREE product lines: Serial NOR Flash (#2 world, 23% share), 32-bit MCU (#1 China, #8 world, GD32 series), and niche DRAM (DDR3/DDR4/LPDDR4X tier-2 challenger). Platform: "存储+控制+传感+模拟."

**Q1 2026 inflection (一季报, Apr 29 2026):** Revenue ¥41.88亿 (+119% YoY, +77% QoQ). 归母净利 ¥14.61亿 (+523% YoY, +159% QoQ). **Gross margin 57.08%** (+19.64pp YoY, +12.17pp QoQ). **Operating margin 41.05%.** Net margin 35.16%. Cash flow/NI ratio 1.22 (¥17.83亿 operating cash vs ¥14.61亿 NI — "真金白银式" cash-backed profits, 东方财富). Inventory ¥34亿, flat QoQ = supply-constrained, not building.

**Why margins exploded:** GigaDevice is fabless — buys DRAM wafers from CXMT (长鑫科技). DRAM selling prices spiked +57% ASP QoQ (SemiAnalysis on CXMT), but GigaDevice's procurement contracts with CXMT have a lag. GD locked in foundry costs at older rates while selling into a market where niche DDR4/NAND prices are rocketing (+120% on 2D NAND). The 57% gross margin is a PROCUREMENT-LAG WINDFALL — real and cash-backed, but time-limited. When CXMT reprices (2026 procurement raised ¥11.61亿→¥57.1亿, ~5x), input costs catch up and the spread compresses. OP margin modeled declining from 41% peak toward 28-30% steady-state as foundry costs reprice.

**CXMT relationship:** GigaDevice holds 1.80% of CXMT (¥23亿 invested, 互动易). Is CXMT's largest niche-DRAM design partner and customer. "设计+制造" domestic co-model. 2026 CXMT foundry procurement: ¥57.1亿 full year; 1H26 alone ¥15.47亿 ($221M), already exceeding all of 2025 spend. CXMT IPO (STAR Market, ~$27-50B MC) makes GD's 1.80% stake a mark-to-market asset (runs through 其他权益工具投资, no direct NI impact per GD's 股价异动公告).

## Rule 9 Test

"If GigaDevice disappeared, could niche DDR4/NOR/MCU still be sourced?" YES.
- Niche DRAM: Winbond (華邦 #1 NOR, also DDR4), Nanya (南亞, pure DRAM), Powerchip (力晶), ESMT (晶豪), ISSI
- NOR Flash: Winbond (#1, 27%), Macronix, MXIC
- MCU: STM, NXP, Renesas, domestic Arm MCU vendors

GigaDevice is **ECOSYSTEM BETA**, not BOTTLENECK ALPHA. It rides the same memory shortage the book captures more directly through the oligopoly (MU/SKH/Samsung). The majors CAUSE the niche scarcity by vacating DDR4; GD catches the spillover. High-quality beneficiary, but owns no physics-layer chokepoint.

## Key Risks

1. **Procurement-lag compression (-1):** The ¥57.1亿 2026 CXMT procurement (vs ¥11.61亿 2025) means input costs are repricing NOW. GM will compress from 57% peak as CXMT passes through higher foundry charges.

2. **CXMT dependence (-1):** Fabless = no alternative DRAM foundry if CXMT prioritizes its own-brand or government redirects to HBM. Single-supplier risk at the manufacturing level.

3. **A-share pricing regime (-1):** At ¥671 the stock trades at ~66x 2026E PE (招商证券 ¥67.6亿 NI). 大股东减持 flagged. Market is in "FOMO状态" (东方财富: "高度亢奋"). Under 20x-OP discipline, deeply negative EV.

4. **Shortage-conditional margins (-1):** The 57% GM is a memory-shortage windfall, not a structural moat. When DDR4 supply normalizes (as CXMT expands + big-3 potentially re-enter), the tier-2 premium compresses. GigaDevice's 30%+ higher cost-per-bit vs majors (SemiAnalysis) means it's the first to lose margin when pricing normalizes.
