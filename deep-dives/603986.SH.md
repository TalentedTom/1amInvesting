## Supercycle: Memory Upcycle + China Localization (DB10 adjacency via niche DRAM)

## Core Thesis: Fabless Niche-Memory + MCU Platform Riding the Biggest Pricing Ramp in Decades

兆易创新 is China's only fabless IC designer ranked top-10 globally across THREE product lines: Serial NOR Flash (#2 world, 23% share), 32-bit MCU (#1 China, #8 world, GD32 series), and niche DRAM (DDR3/DDR4/LPDDR4X tier-2 challenger). Platform: "存储+控制+传感+模拟."

**Q1 2026 inflection (一季报, Apr 29 2026):** Revenue ¥41.88亿 (+119% YoY, +77% QoQ). 归母净利 ¥14.61亿 (+523% YoY, +159% QoQ). **Gross margin 57.08%** (+19.64pp YoY, +12.17pp QoQ). **Operating margin 41.05%.** Net margin 35.16%. Cash flow/NI ratio 1.22 (¥17.83亿 operating cash vs ¥14.61亿 NI — "真金白银式" cash-backed profits, 东方财富). Inventory ¥34亿, flat QoQ = supply-constrained, not building.

**Why margins exploded:** GigaDevice is fabless — buys DRAM wafers from CXMT (长鑫科技). The niche storage pricing surge is FAR larger than initially modeled. From 36氪/东方财富/ZAKER: DDR4 16Gb up **+1,800%** from early 2025. DDR5 spot **+455%**. Server DDR5 high-end modules **+600%**. Some high-spec products **+10x**. SLC NAND **+50%+** in Q1 alone. NOR Flash **+20-30%** QoQ. GigaDevice locked in CXMT foundry costs at older rates while selling into this 10-18x pricing environment. The 57% gross margin is a PROCUREMENT-LAG WINDFALL — real and cash-backed, but time-limited as CXMT reprices (2026 procurement raised ¥11.61亿→¥57.1亿, ~5x).

**Management 2027 pricing outlook (互动易/东方财富):** "2026年供需偏紧将支撑利基DRAM、SLC NAND、NOR Flash价格续涨；2027年利基DRAM新增产能落地后，价格有望在高位震荡，不排除从峰值向下适当回归，但仍将维持在较高价格区间." Translation: prices continue rising 2026; after new capacity in 2027, prices oscillate from peak but REMAIN AT ELEVATED LEVELS. Management does NOT expect a return to pre-cycle pricing — the floor is structurally higher.

**Why GigaDevice outperforms while global AI stocks sell off (-20-30%):** China domestic insulation. GigaDevice sells into Chinese consumer electronics (智能家电), automotive (汽车电子), IoT (物联网), and industrial — NOT into Western AI datacenters. When SIVE/AAOI/MU sell off on Western AI sentiment (InP fears, sector rotation, KOSPI weakness), GigaDevice is IMMUNE because its demand is domestically driven by 国产替代 (domestic substitution) + niche storage supercycle. The A-share market treats niche storage as a national champion play with sovereignty premium. Different demand curve, different market, different valuation regime. 普冉股份 (688766) up 5.57x, 东芯股份 (688110) up 3.04x in the same period — the entire niche storage sector is outperforming.

**CXMT relationship:** GigaDevice holds 1.80% of CXMT (¥23亿 invested, 互动易). Is CXMT's largest niche-DRAM design partner and customer. "设计+制造" domestic co-model. 2026 CXMT foundry procurement: ¥57.1亿 full year; 1H26 alone ¥15.47亿 ($221M), already exceeding all of 2025 spend. CXMT IPO (STAR Market, ~$27-50B MC) makes GD's 1.80% stake a mark-to-market asset (runs through 其他权益工具投资, no direct NI impact per GD's 股价异动公告).

## Key Risks

1. **Procurement-lag compression (-1):** The ¥57.1亿 2026 CXMT procurement (vs ¥11.61亿 2025) means input costs are repricing NOW. GM will compress from 57% peak as CXMT passes through higher foundry charges. HOWEVER, management says prices "remain at elevated levels" even post-2027 capacity — the floor is higher than prior cycles.

2. **CXMT dependence (-1):** Fabless = no alternative DRAM foundry if CXMT prioritizes its own-brand or government redirects to HBM. Single-supplier risk at the manufacturing level.

3. **A-share pricing regime (-1):** At ¥775 the stock trades at **184.73x rolling P/E** (异常波动公告 Jun 25). CEO 朱一明 reducing 1.6% of shares (¥28亿 cash-out). Stock triggered abnormal trading alert (+20% in 2 days). Under 20x-OP discipline, deeply negative EV. The A-share market uses a fundamentally different valuation framework (70x annualized Q1 NI for a national champion supercycle).

4. **Shortage-conditional margins (-1):** The 57% GM is a memory-shortage windfall, not a structural moat. Management acknowledges margins "may moderately decline from peak" post-2027 but expects "elevated levels" — partial mitigation but still a risk if the pricing surge reverses more sharply than guided.
