## Supercycle: Germanium — The Deepest Layer of the Fiber Optic Supply Chain

## What Yunnan Germanium Does

Vertically integrated germanium producer — mines, refines, and processes germanium into four product lines:

**1. Fiber-Grade Germanium Products (~44% of global Ge revenue):**
- Germanium tetrachloride (GeCl4) — critical dopant for fiber optic cable preforms
- Without GeO2/GeCl4 doping, fiber optic glass cannot bend light efficiently
- Every meter of fiber optic cable requires germanium

**2. Infrared-Grade Germanium Products (premium, +51% growth):**
- Germanium single crystals and blanks
- Germanium lenses for thermal imaging cameras
- Infrared optical systems for defense/surveillance
- Space-grade germanium for satellite solar cells

**3. Photovoltaic-Grade Germanium Products:**
- Solar germanium wafers for multi-junction solar cells
- Primarily space/satellite applications (highest efficiency solar)

**4. Compound Semiconductor Materials (small but strategic):**
- GaAs (gallium arsenide) wafers
- InP (indium phosphide) wafers
- Positions company in semiconductor substrate market alongside AXTI
