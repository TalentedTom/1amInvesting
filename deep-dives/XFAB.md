## Supercycle: SiC Power Semis + Silicon Photonics / CPO (Specialty Foundry)

## Core Thesis: The SOI.PA of Foundries — Legacy Drag Masking Two Structural Growth Engines

X-Fab is a specialty analog/mixed-signal foundry with 6 fabs across Germany, France, Malaysia, and the US. The market sees an $873M revenue foundry with declining automotive exposure, 8.8% EBIT margin, and CEO transition. What the market DOESN'T see:

1. **Only high-volume SiC foundry in the US** (one of only TWO globally — X-Fab + Episil)
2. **NVIDIA evaluating X-Fab's photonixFAB platform** for silicon photonics CPO (transceivers + optical switches)
3. **Dual government backstop** — EU CHIPS Act €128M + US CHIPS Act $50M
4. **SiC revenue +152% YoY Q1 2026**, wafer shipments nearly tripled

Western parallel: **SOI.PA (Soitec)** — both European specialty fabs, both legacy auto drag masking photonics growth, both ~1.3x P/B, both receiving CHIPS Act funding. SOI.PA at €154 is 18 months ahead on the same trajectory X-Fab is starting.

## Vector 1: SiC Foundry Duopoly (PRIMARY GROWTH ENGINE)

Only TWO professional SiC foundries worldwide: X-Fab and Episil. X-Fab's Lubbock, Texas fab is the ONLY high-volume SiC foundry in the US. US CHIPS Act $50M specifically for this facility — "bolster supply resiliency for critical infrastructure markets."

SiC revenue FY2025: ~$231M. Q4 +77% YoY. Q1 2026: +152% YoY, wafer shipments nearly tripled. Converting Lubbock from legacy to SiC production base.

Confirmed customers:
- **Navitas (NVTS):** SiC chips fabricated at X-Fab (1.2kV for AI data centers, 2kV+ for grid)
- **Power Integrations (POWI):** PCN-22181 explicitly names X-Fab as foundry
- **Infineon supply chain:** 800V power semi upcycle (dual price increases May 2026)

Every AI data center needs SiC power devices for 800V power delivery. The same Infineon price increase cycle driving power semi demand = more wafers through X-Fab's SiC foundry.

## Vector 2: Silicon Photonics / CPO — NVIDIA + Nokia HVM Qualification

"NVIDIA is responsible for testing the photonixFAB technology as an end user. The company will use the photonixFAB platform to generate layouts for transceivers and optical switches and will evaluate the demonstrators on its testbed."

NVIDIA and Nokia are now qualifying X-Fab for silicon photonics HVM (per Serenity, May 2026). GFS deck lists X-Fab in the SiPh foundry ecosystem. EU has provided **€47.6M specifically for X-Fab's silicon photonics supply chain** — a third government funding line dedicated to SiPh capability.

X-Fab leads the photonixFAB consortium — the market hasn't connected this to NVIDIA's CPO push or Nokia's optical networking needs. Same dynamic as TSEM before Serenity went long.

Current: ~$5-6M NRE (development phase). Product revenue expected 2027-2028. Microsystems + photonics hit RECORD $33.7M Q1 2026 (+42% YoY).

If X-Fab qualifies as a SiPh foundry for NVIDIA CPO and Nokia optical, they become the THIRD SiPh foundry alongside TSEM and GFS — and the only one with simultaneous SiC + SiPh + MEMS capability.

## Vector 3: 800V Power Semis for AI Data Centers

Infineon just issued 2nd price increase in 3 months (May 26, effective July 1). CICC: "upward cycle of the industry has arrived." Every GPU rack needs power delivery — SiC is the material of choice for 800V+ architectures.

X-Fab doesn't make the chips — it MANUFACTURES them for fabless designers (Navitas, POWI, etc). More power semi demand = more wafer starts at X-Fab regardless of which chip designer wins.
