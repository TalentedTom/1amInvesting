## Supercycle: SiC Power Semis + Silicon Photonics / CPO (Specialty Foundry)

## Core Thesis: The SOI.PA of Foundries

The market sees an $873M-revenue specialty foundry with declining automotive exposure, 8.8% EBIT margin, and a CEO transition. What it doesn't see: **one of only two professional SiC foundries on earth** (X-Fab + Episil), with the **only high-volume SiC fab in the US** (Lubbock, $50M US CHIPS Act); **NVIDIA and Nokia qualifying the photonixFAB platform for silicon photonics HVM**; ~$230M+ in government grants across three dedicated funding lines (~18% of market cap in funded capex); and growth segments compounding under the auto drag — SiC +152% YoY with wafer shipments nearly tripled, microsystems + photonics at a record $33.7M (+42%).

The Western parallel is Soitec: both European specialty fabs, both legacy drag masking a photonics engine, both ~1.3x P/B at the entry point, both CHIPS-funded. SOI.PA is 18 months ahead on the same trajectory. Structural downside protection: dual-government backstop plus below-replacement-cost book value — an acquirer or a state cannot rebuild this capacity for the price of the equity.
