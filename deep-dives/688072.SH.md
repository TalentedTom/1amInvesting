## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Piotech is a qualified domestic deposition platform with production PECVD, ALD and gap-fill equipment. Memory layer growth and more complex logic process flows increase deposition work, while localization creates share opportunities. Wafer bonding is established, but die-to-wafer hybrid bonding and several related tools remain in customer validation. International suppliers and competing domestic tools prevent a global sole-source classification.

Fab capital budgets → qualified deposition/bonding tools → customer installation and process acceptance → recognized equipment revenue and installed-base services. Domestic qualification creates a local bottleneck, while global replacement vendors remain. More film steps can raise equipment intensity, but adding both a spending-growth multiplier and the same process-intensity uplift would double count demand.
