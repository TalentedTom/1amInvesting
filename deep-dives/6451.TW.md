## ShunSin in the Supply Chain

```
NVIDIA/Broadcom CPO Switch
  └── needs optical packaging/assembly → ShunSin (6451.TW) FAU alignment
      └── packages CW lasers from → SIVE / LITE / COHR
          └── on SiPh chips from → TSEM foundry
              └── tested by → AEHR WLBI
```

ShunSin is the ASSEMBLY layer — it takes the components (lasers, SiPh chips, fibers) and assembles them into working CPO modules. Every company below ShunSin in the stack (SIVE, TSEM, AEHR) feeds it components.
