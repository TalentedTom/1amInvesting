## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (CPO/NPO Optical Engine Packaging)

## Core Thesis: TSMC COUPE Backend Optical-Engine Bottleneck

ShunSin publicly confirmed it is working with TSMC's COUPE silicon-photonics platform for backend optical-engine packaging: FAU (Fiber Array Unit) alignment, grating coupling, integration, and verification. Chairman Shang-Yi Chiang (former head of R&D at TSMC) confirmed this directly. Two additional former TSMC executives joined the board. This is not a normal customer-supplier relationship — TSMC is seeding its CPO packaging partner with its own people.

**Product roadmap (management confirmed Jun 2026):**
- 51.2T CPO: **pilot production** (current-gen Tomahawk/Spectrum-X class)
- 102.4T CPO: **samples shipped to clients** (next-gen, 2x current)
- 12.8T NPO: in development
- OCS (Optical Circuit Switches): in development
- 3.2T optical-engine packaging: in development

**Why this matters:** FAU alignment fixes fibers to silicon photonics dies at nanometer precision. This is NOT commodity OSAT packaging. The industry has repeatedly named FAU as the CPO bottleneck. ShunSin owns this step on TSMC's flagship CPO platform.

**Parent anchor:** Foxconn (2317.TW) all-optical CPO switch cabinets shipped early to NVIDIA, 2026-27 forecast revised to 50,000+ units (5x original), >15% of FII revenue. Foxconn = NVIDIA's primary manufacturing partner; ShunSin is the packaging arm.

**Capex:** TWD 4-5B for 2026-2027, mainly CPO automation and advanced packaging. Vietnam plant output by end-2026, ramp 2027.
