## Core Thesis: Foxconn's CPO Packaging Arm

ShunSin is Foxconn's semiconductor packaging subsidiary executing the HARDEST part of CPO assembly — FAU (fiber array unit) optical alignment. Aligning optical fibers to silicon photonics chips at nanometer precision is NOT commodity packaging. When CPO scales, margins transform from 15-16% (commodity OSAT) to 30-35% (optical alignment).

```
NVIDIA/Broadcom CPO Switch
  └── needs optical packaging/assembly → ShunSin (6451.TW) FAU alignment
```
