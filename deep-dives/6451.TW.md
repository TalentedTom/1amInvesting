## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (CPO Optical Packaging) + SiP for AI Smartphones

## Core Thesis: Foxconn's CPO Packaging Arm

ShunSin is Foxconn's semiconductor packaging subsidiary executing the hardest part of CPO assembly — FAU optical alignment, fixing fibers to silicon photonics at nanometer precision. That step is NOT commodity packaging, and the industry's own delay discourse this month (FAU named as the CPO bottleneck) independently confirmed where the difficulty lives. When CPO scales, ShunSin's margin structure transforms:

| Product | GM |
|---------|-----|
| Commodity OSAT | 15-16% |
| 400G/800G optical | 20-24% |
| CPO FAU alignment | 30-35% |
| Blended at maturity | 25-30% |

**Parent-level anchor (June 2026, Foxconn's own disclosure — hardest evidence class):** all-optical CPO switch cabinets shipped EARLY to NVIDIA; 2026-27 shipment forecast revised UP to 50,000+ units (5x the original 10,000); double-digit gross margins; expected to exceed **15% of Foxconn Industrial Internet revenue** — "a second growth curve." CPO is a parent-level strategic priority, and ShunSin is the packaging arm it runs through.
