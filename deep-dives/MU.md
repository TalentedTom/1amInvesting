## Core Thesis: The US Memory Champion, Now Shipping HBM4 to Rubin

Micron posted the most extraordinary quarter in memory industry history and then guided above it. The thesis upgrade that matters most: **HBM4 volume shipments to NVIDIA Vera Rubin are confirmed and underway** — directly refuting the channel-check claim that MU had no Rubin share (the miss that preceded the stock's triple, now the book's standing source-calibration case). Structural position: the US memory champion — CHIPS-funded fabs (Idaho mid-2027), American-premium procurement, no union profit-sharing drag, diversified DRAM+HBM+NAND. Penalties that keep this honest: #3 in HBM, banned in China (a permanent TAM haircut Samsung monetizes instead), and mega-cap commodity perception.
