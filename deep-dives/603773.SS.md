## Company Description

Woge Optoelectronics (沃格光电) is a Jiangxi-based glass processing company with three business segments: (1) traditional display panel glass processing (core revenue today), (2) new display business (Mini LED/Micro LED glass substrates), (3) glass circuit plates (GCP) for CPO, advanced packaging, and 5G/6G applications through subsidiary Hubei Tonggewei.

The CPO connection is real: Woge has built a 100K sqm/year TGV (Through-Glass Via) production line, has completed 1.6T CPO glass substrate small batch sampling, and is cooperating with "国内H公司" (likely Huawei) on glass-based optical module substrates.

## Why LPK.DE = 104🔥 and Woge = 30 FAIL

Both are "glass substrate for CPO" companies. The difference is moat quality:

LPK.DE's LIDE: Molecular modification of glass using laser-induced deep etching. Fundamentally different from mechanical or conventional laser processing. Zero mechanical stress. Zero cracks. Physics breakthrough. Sole-source. 8+ independent confirmations. SKC, Intel, TSMC as customers.

Woge's GCP/TGV: Conventional glass circuit plate manufacturing. Multiple companies can do TGV (Corning, AGC, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, etc.). Engineering capability, not physics monopoly. Still sampling, not shipping.
