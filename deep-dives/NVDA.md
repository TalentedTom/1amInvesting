## Supercycle: DB1 AI Infrastructure (GPU Compute + Networking + CUDA)

## Core Thesis: The Toll Booth of the AI Revolution

NVIDIA is the foundational layer: 80-90% of AI training compute, the networking that clusters it (NVLink, InfiniBand, Spectrum-X), and the software moat that locks it (CUDA, 5M+ developers, switching cost measured in years of retraining). Jensen: AI capex "$3-4 trillion annually" by 2030. Not a framework chokepoint — at $5T and 50+ analysts there is no edge and no asymmetry — but THE platform every position in the book feeds on: SIVE's lasers connect its GPUs, FOCI aligns its fibers, ShunSin packages its switch optics, AEHR burns in its photonics, SKH/Samsung/MU fill its HBM sockets, HPS steps down its power.

**The equity web (the circularity, mapped):** NVIDIA has locked its own supply chain and demand chain with capital — $2B each into LITE and COHR (CPO laser supply pacts), $2B into NBIS (preferential Vera Rubin access), a multi-year co-design memory partnership with SK Hynix, and a $10B stake in Anthropic. The toll booth owns pieces of the roads on both sides.
