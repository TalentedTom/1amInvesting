## Supercycle: DB5 Advanced Packaging — Hybrid Bonding

## Core Thesis: The Hybrid Bonding Equipment Monopoly

BESI makes the equipment that bonds semiconductor dies together at the atomic level. Their hybrid bonding tools achieve <10nm precision for direct copper-to-copper + oxide-to-oxide bonding — replacing traditional microbump/solder joints that max out at 40-55μm pitch. This is a 10x+ improvement in interconnect density with dramatically lower energy per bit (<0.05 pJ/bit).

### Why Hybrid Bonding Is the Next Packaging Transition

```
Current (microbump/TC bonding):
  40-55μm pitch solder joints
  Sufficient for: HBM3, HBM3E, current CoWoS
  Limits: thermal, density ceiling, resistance

Hybrid bonding (BESI's technology):
  Direct Cu-to-Cu + oxide bonding
  <10nm accuracy
  10x+ interconnect density
  Required for: HBM5, SoIC, 3D chiplets, Foveros Direct, CPO COUPE

Timeline:
  2026: Major qualification year (ALL memory + logic companies qualifying)
  2027: Mainstream volume production begins
  2028-2030: HBM5 + SoIC + CPO = full ramp
```
