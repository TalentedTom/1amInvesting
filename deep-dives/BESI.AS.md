## Supercycle: DB5 AI Chip Packaging (Hybrid Bonding / Advanced Assembly)

## Core Thesis: The Tollgate for 3D Chip Stacking

BESI holds a near-monopoly in hybrid bonding equipment — the assembly technology that eliminates micro-bumps between chip stacks, using direct copper-to-copper interconnection at sub-micron pitch. Every HBM4 stack, every 3D chiplet integration, every CPO optical engine assembly that uses hybrid bonding runs through BESI tools. Applied Materials bought 9% of BESI (April 2025) and extended a co-development agreement for the industry's first fully integrated die-based hybrid bonding solution. Lam Research and Applied Materials both reportedly explored multi-billion-euro acquisition offers in March 2026.

Q1 2026 orders €269.7M, more than doubled YoY. ~50% of Q4 2025 orders for the most advanced hybrid bonding systems. Backlog ~€400M with 6-month lead times. GM 65%+ — industry-leading for assembly equipment, ASML-class pricing power. Q2 2026 guided +30-40% vs Q1 (€240-259M). Management: "the pace of hybrid bonding adoption is increasing as we approach the timing for many new AI-related products anticipated in the 2027 to 2030 period."

The China vector (Zephyr/Citrini): China revenue 28% (Q1 2025) → 46% (Q1 2026), €40.5M→€84.2M in five quarters (+108%). Huawei adopting hybrid bonding for logic folding. The Chinese AI packaging chain building in parallel without TSMC access, using BESI equipment.

## Cross-Book Read-Throughs

**LPK.DE (#2):** BESI does hybrid bonding on organic substrates TODAY. When glass substrates arrive (LPK LIDE + TSMC CoPoS), BESI tools will do hybrid bonding ON glass. The two are complements: LPK drills the holes, BESI bonds the chips. Both benefit from the packaging transition.

**SHMD (#49):** Schmid metallizes the glass that LPK drills. BESI then bonds chips onto the metallized glass substrate. Three-company workflow: LPK → SHMD → BESI.

**AIXA (#33):** Huawei adopting hybrid bonding for logic folding means Huawei AI chips need III-V lasers for optical interconnect. AIXA's Source Photonics MOCVD orders feed the upstream of whatever Huawei packages with BESI equipment.

**AEHR (#19):** Both are equipment companies benefiting from advanced packaging. AEHR does burn-in/test, BESI does assembly. Different steps, same ramp.

## Key Risks

1. **China export control (-1):** 46% of revenue from China and growing. Any EU/US restriction on hybrid bonding equipment exports to China = immediate revenue loss. The Huawei adoption vector IS the risk vector — same revenue, two readings.

2. **Cyclicality (-1):** Semi equipment is the most cyclical layer. 2023-2024 was a trough. Orders can drop 50%+ in a downturn. The 65% GM provides cushion but revenue volatility is real.

3. **ASMPT competition (-1):** ASMPT (522.HK) has competing hybrid bonding tools. Currently behind on throughput and precision, but investing aggressively. If ASMPT closes the gap, BESI's monopoly pricing erodes. Applied Materials' 9% stake is partially a hedge — they're co-developing with BESI while also building their own assembly capabilities.
