### Pillar 2 — EPYC CPU (Server Processors)

CPU renaissance driven by agentic AI. CPU:GPU ratio shifting from 1:8 to 1:1 to 4:1 (Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan confirmed, Morgan Stanley modeled, Lisa Su stated). Jefferies Taiwan checks: server CPU market sustaining 20-30% unit growth. Venice/Verano 6th Gen EPYC launching. AMD projects $120B server CPU TAM by 2030.

AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, Tencent all expanded EPYC-powered instances.

### Pillar 3 — CPO Pioneer via GFS

AMD is the FIRST major chip company to commit to CPO for its accelerators. MI500 uses GlobalFoundries Fotonix for co-packaged optics. GFS ecosystem slide shows SIVE and LITE as the ONLY two laser providers. This validates the CPO transition timeline for the entire portfolio.
