## Executive verdict

紫光股份 is no longer appropriately described as a generic downstream ICT equipment vendor. The company has become a credible Chinese AI-infrastructure platform through its controlling ownership of 新华三 / H3C, with meaningful positions in data-center switches, enterprise networking, x86 servers, storage, security, cloud infrastructure and increasingly full-rack AI systems.

The old 50% / FAIL posture was too harsh. It correctly recognized that H3C is competitive rather than irreplaceable, but it underweighted four developments:

1. **AI-compute demand is converting into reported revenue and normalized profit growth.**
2. **H3C has commercial 800G, CPO and scale-up products rather than only laboratory projects.**
3. **紫光股份 increased its H3C ownership from 81% to 87.98%.**
4. **The historical put-liability interest burden ended, raising the recurring earnings floor.**

The correct classification is a **Path B platform**, not a chokepoint. The company can compound with China’s sovereign AI and domestic-substitution cycle, but it does not have monopoly economics and faces material competition, working-capital intensity, balance-sheet risk and dilution.

**Recommendation:** promote Base from **50% to 67%** and use the TAM-refreshed quarterly revenue path below. The approved update raises 2026 and 2027 revenue while retaining every previously sanctioned operating-margin assumption. Do **not** raise gross-margin assumptions until the full H1 2026 report confirms a genuine percentage-margin inflection.

# 1. Company overview

紫光股份 is a full-stack Chinese ICT infrastructure provider. Through H3C and other subsidiaries, it sells enterprise and data-center switches, routers, WLAN, x86 and AI servers, storage, cybersecurity, private/hybrid cloud, intelligent-computing systems, data-center orchestration, CPO/scale-up products and distribution services.

The company describes its coverage as “云—网—安—算—存—端”: cloud, network, security, compute, storage and endpoints.

The economic engine is H3C. In 2025:

| Metric | 2025 |
|---|---:|
| 紫光股份 revenue | RMB 96.75B |
| H3C revenue | RMB 75.98B |
| H3C net profit | RMB 3.15B |
| H3C domestic government/enterprise revenue | RMB 65.85B |
| H3C international revenue | RMB 4.62B |

H3C represents the majority of consolidated revenue and substantially all of the strategic AI-infrastructure value.

# 3. AI infrastructure and product roadmap

The strongest thesis upgrade is that H3C is increasingly selling an integrated AI-factory architecture rather than only conventional enterprise equipment.

Confirmed product and deployment evidence includes:

- **UniPoD S80000** supernode systems supporting 32 to 1,024 accelerators.
- **800G AI switching** and DDC lossless data-center networking.
- A **51.2T CPO silicon-photonics data-center switch** that completed interoperability tests with multiple 800G modules and test equipment.
- Strategic-customer POC completion and **batch deployment** of the 51.2T CPO switch.
- A domestic-GPU **32-card scale-up node** with POC and small-volume shipment capability.
- 64-card and 256-card nodes in board bring-up.

This places H3C at the Chinese AI-factory platform layer: compute, networking, storage, security, orchestration and interconnect. Its moat is system architecture, product breadth, installed base and localization—not exclusive materials or manufacturing IP.

# 6. Revenue versus margin: what is proven

Q1 gross margin was approximately 12.88%, versus roughly 15.67% in Q1 2025. Gross-margin percentage declined, even while operating profit and adjusted net profit improved.

The preview’s phrase “毛利水平持续提升” may refer to a higher **absolute gross-profit level**, not necessarily a higher percentage margin.

### Model treatment

- Revenue path raised.
- Operating-margin path raised gradually.
- Gross-margin percentage not raised yet.
- Interest-expense elimination is treated as recurring earnings-floor improvement, not gross-margin expansion.
- H3C ownership uplift is reflected through attribution and earnings.

The full H1 report must confirm gross margin, operating expense, cash flow and inventory before a higher margin ceiling is sanctioned.

### TAM validation — 2026-07-11 approved revenue refresh

- IDC reported that China's accelerated-server market reached USD 16B in H1 2025, more than doubling year over year, with Inspur, H3C and Lenovo together holding nearly 50% of sales. IDC expects the market to exceed USD 140B by 2029.
- H3C's Q1 2026 revenue increased 45.1%; domestic government/enterprise revenue increased 55.6%; international revenue increased 56.6%.
- China data-center switching grew materially faster than the overall switching market, while H3C held 33.1% of data-center switching and 36.1% of enterprise switching in 2025.
- H3C's commercial 800G, DDC, CPO/NPO and UniPoD supernode deployments support a higher revenue path, but finished systems remain competitive and do not yet demonstrate scarcity pricing.
- Severe shortages remain concentrated in upstream accelerators, server CPUs, HBM/DRAM and selected high-speed components. H3C benefits through allocation and delivery capability, but current financials show volume capture rather than proven consolidated margin expansion.

# 11. Catalysts and monitoring

### Near-term
- Full H1 2026 report: Q2 revenue, gross margin, OP, cash flow, inventory and minority interest.
- Full six-month benefit from 87.98% H3C ownership in H2.
- Final private-placement share count and debt reduction.
- Intelligent-computing orders, AI-server mix and data-center switch growth.
- 51.2T CPO deployment and 32/64/256-card scale-up progression.

### Upgrade triggers
- Q2 revenue above roughly RMB 35B.
- H1 gross-margin percentage stabilizes or improves.
- Cash flow improves despite rapid growth.
- Repeat commercial CPO/scale-up deployments.
- Placement smaller than maximum or strongly de-risks debt.
- 2027 OP margin above 5.0–5.5%.

### Downgrade triggers
- Growth driven mainly by low-margin distribution/server mix.
- Gross margin remains below 13%.
- Inventory/receivables materially outgrow sales.
- Near-maximum dilution without adequate debt reduction.
- AI products remain POC rather than volume.
- Competition compresses pricing.
