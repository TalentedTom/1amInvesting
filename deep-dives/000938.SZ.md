## Company Description

Unisplendour is China's leading IT infrastructure company through its core subsidiary New H3C (新华三, 81→88% owned). Products include ethernet switches (#2 China, 31.1% share), x86 servers (#3 China, 12.6% share), and networking equipment. H3C developed the world's first 51.2T 800G CPO silicon photonics switch, 6 months ahead of international competitors. Serves 260+ Fortune 500 companies. Revenue CNY ~88B ($12B+).

## Why It FAILS

1. DOWNSTREAM, NOT UPSTREAM: Unisplendour MAKES switches and servers that USE CPO components. It BUYS from the supply chain the framework invests in (lasers, substrates, foundries, test). The framework invests in bottleneck SUPPLIERS, not end-product CONSUMERS.

2. NOT A MONOPOLY: Competes with Huawei (#1), Inspur, Lenovo, Dell/HPE in every segment. #2 in switches and #3 in servers is strong but NOT sole-source. No physics moat. No patent barrier.

3. 82% DEBT RATIO: Extremely leveraged. Short-term borrowings CNY 14.84B (+83.5% YoY). Financial risk is elevated. One downturn and the balance sheet is stressed.

4. NO ASYMMETRY: At CNY 49.60, ceiling midpoint CNY 67.50 = 1.36x ratio. Below meaningful entry threshold for a non-monopoly company. Market already fairly pricing the AI server/switch demand.
