## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Higher memory stacks and more complex logic require additional etch, deposition and inspection steps. AMEC has production qualifications that are difficult to reproduce quickly, while localization increases its available customer wallet. Lam, TEL, Applied Materials, NAURA, Piotech and domestic specialists remain capable competitors. Its value is qualified process access and expanding product scope; coverage of a process market is not ownership of that market.

Fab capital budget → process-step demand → qualified tool vendor → delivery/acceptance → installed-base support. Multi-patterning and three-dimensional memory increase process intensity, but complexity may also delay customer production. Advanced-packaging etch stays in etch and packaging deposition stays in thin film; no duplicate packaging revenue vector.
