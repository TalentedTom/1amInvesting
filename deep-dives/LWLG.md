## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (Layer -1 Material — EO Polymer for Modulators)

## TAM Expansion Lens

**Q1 (Today):** EO polymer material for 1.6T/3.2T datacenter optical modulators. Replaces silicon carrier-based modulators hitting bandwidth walls at 400G/lane.

**Q2 (3-5 Years):** Coherent DCI (ZR/ZR+), telecom transport, 6.4T+ datacenter. Every modulator platform that needs >100 GHz bandwidth AND low drive voltage transitions to Pockels-based materials. Perkinamine competes with TFLN but has foundry integration advantages (spin-coat vs smart-cut).

**Q3 (New Markets):** Quantum photonics, space optical comms, sensing, test & measurement. Any application requiring ultra-fast, ultra-low-power light modulation.

**The Ford Principle applied:** LWLG isn't selling "polymer ink." It's selling the universal high-speed modulation primitive for all post-silicon-carrier photonics. Every speed generation beyond 200G/lane increases demand for Pockels-based modulation. Silicon carrier-based modulators are the copper cables of photonics — they work today but physics limits them. Perkinamine is the optical transition for the modulator layer.

## Key Data

- Sole developer of Perkinamine®, a patented EO polymer using the Pockels Effect
- Fabless IP/material model — sells polymer "ink" + licenses know-how to foundries
- Rule 3 exempt: IP-licensing model immune to geographic pricing culture
- Stock: $13.60. Market cap: ~$2.1B. 52-week: $0.79-$14.82 (+1,625%)

## THREE VALIDATED INTEGRATION PATHS (Critical Discovery)

**Path 1 — Tower Semiconductor (TSEM):**
- Definitive agreement to integrate 110 GHz+ electro-optic polymer modulators into Tower's high-volume PH18 silicon photonics platform
- Full wafer tape-out launched Jan 2026, chips returning Q2 2026
- Tower is our existing portfolio position (TSEM 66)

**Path 2 — GlobalFoundries:**
- EO polymer modulators now integrated into the GDSFactory process design kit that supports GlobalFoundries' silicon photonics platform for 200G/400G per lane data center and AI builds
- GlobalFoundries is optimizing its silicon photonics process to meet Lightwave Logic's next-generation architecture requirements
- Standard PDK means any chip designer can drop LWLG modulators into their layouts

**Path 3 — Marvell/Polariton (NEW — PhotonCap discovery):**
- Perkinamine is the EO polymer INSIDE Polariton's POH (Plasmonic-Organic Hybrid) modulators
- Marvell acquired Polariton (April 22, 2026) for its 3.2T roadmap
- Marvell bought the device but CANNOT make the fuel — Perkinamine is patented, molecular-structure-based, NOT a replaceable commodity
- Partnership formalized 2022. 400 Gbps POH MZM demonstrated 2025
- Marvell's 3.2T roadmap depends on this material

## Technical Validation

- Perkinamine demonstrated 200 Gbps per lane and passed Telcordia 85/85 reliability testing (85°C, 85% humidity)
- 110 GHz+ bandwidth (vs silicon carrier ~50 GHz limitation)
- Pockels Effect = pure phase modulation, no chirp (superior to silicon plasma dispersion)
- CEO outlined 2026 priorities including advancing stage-three programs toward qualification and stage four, with operational preparation for a potential 2027 production ramp
