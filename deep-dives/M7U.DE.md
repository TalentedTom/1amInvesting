## FAIL — Distressed Company in Restructuring

## What Manz Is

German high-tech equipment manufacturer (founded 1987, Reutlingen). Two segments: Mobility & Battery Solutions (lithium-ion battery production equipment) and Industry Solutions (semiconductor packaging, display, automation). Operations across Germany, Hungary, Italy, Slovakia, China, Taiwan.

## What Happened

Manz is in deep financial distress:
- Revenue declining (€277M → €269M → ~€204M trailing)
- EPS: -€4.40 TTM. Consensus next year: -€1.00.
- Stock down 92% from 200-day moving average
- Selling battery cell equipment business (core segment)
- Tesla acquired 300+ employees from Reutlingen site
- Greatech Technology (Malaysia) bought 20% at €5.80/share (Oct 2024) — stock now €1.17
- Classified as "Value Trap" by Stockopedia
- IPCEI funding approved (€70M) but "not yet been drawn"
- Market cap ~€12M on ~€204M revenue = 0.06x P/S

## The CoPoS Angle (Why It Was In the Portfolio)

The ONE interesting technology: **CoPoS (Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate)** — panel-level packaging as an alternative to TSMC's CoWoS. Manz set up a Semiconductor Innovation R&D Center in Taoyuan, Taiwan for CoPoS RDL equipment.

If CoWoS is the bottleneck (SemiVision confirmed), panel-level alternatives COULD be valuable. CoPoS processes redistribution layers on large panels instead of wafers, potentially offering higher throughput and lower cost.

**BUT:** The technology is pre-commercial. The company is in financial distress. The market cap is €12M. Even if CoPoS works, Manz may not survive long enough to commercialize it. The Greatech investor at €5.80 has lost 80% in 18 months.
