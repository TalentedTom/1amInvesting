## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Higher server power and rapidly changing accelerator loads increase demand for reliable filtering and short-duration power support. Jianghai combines aluminium, film and supercapacitor products with upstream materials and customer qualification. That breadth improves its ability to win power-system designs. Competing capacitor technologies and vendors remain available, and supercapacitors do not replace every battery or power-storage function.

Electrode foil and carbon/material preparation → capacitor cell and module manufacture → customer power-supply qualification → industrial, automotive, grid and server deployment. Internal material transfers are eliminated; only external product revenue enters valuation.
