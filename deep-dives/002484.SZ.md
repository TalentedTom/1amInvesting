## Core Thesis: AI Rack Power Turns Capacitors Into Power-Stability Bottlenecks

AI racks push power density to levels where capacitors shift from boring passives to critical power-stability infrastructure. Jianghai is China's leading aluminum-electrolytic capacitor platform, with film capacitors and supercapacitors as second-growth engines. GB300 NVL72 rack power raises transient power-stability requirements, making supercapacitors and BBU part of the Energy Storage Tray architecture.

**The caution:** AI-server revenue is still tiny. Company disclosed Q1 2026 supercapacitor revenue was ¥1.06亿 (7.66% of company revenue), and AI-server power application was only ~¥1,200万. Company warned investors to be rational. Stock +264% from April to June, 137.72x TTM PE, above industry average. No undisclosed major orders.

**Rule 6/9:** FAIL global chokepoint (Nichicon, Nippon Chemi-Con, Rubycon, Panasonic, TDK/KEMET all exist). PASS China domestic capacitor leader with AI power optionality. Classification: 📊 BENEFICIARY.
