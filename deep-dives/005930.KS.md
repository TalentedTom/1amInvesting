## Upgrade Triggers

1. HBM4E qualifies at NVIDIA, Google, AMD, or major custom ASIC customer.
2. Samsung discloses HBM4E volume shipment timing.
3. HBM4E yield reaches 80%+ mature-yield territory.
4. D1d passes PRA in November.
5. Samsung discloses HBM5 design wins or HBM5 sample timing.
6. Google TPU I/O die contract is confirmed.
7. Samsung bundles HBM + 2nm foundry + packaging for a major AI ASIC.
8. DS Memory OP beats the quarterly ramp path without consumer-memory weakness.
9. HBM market share moves materially above current assumptions.
10. Foundry profitability arrives earlier than modeled.

The most important trigger remains:

> **HBM4E customer qualification and volume shipment schedule.**

## Downgrade Triggers

1. HBM4E customer qualification slips.
2. Reliability-test yield stalls below mature levels.
3. D1d PRA is delayed.
4. SK Hynix / Micron retain the entire HBM4E high-end share.
5. Google TPU I/O die goes to TSMC / Intel / other partner.
6. Samsung foundry losses widen despite AI-chip optionality.
7. China memory demand shifts materially to CXMT faster than expected.
8. DRAM / NAND price increases reverse before 2028.
9. Chaebol governance or capital allocation worsens.
10. MX / display / legacy businesses dilute the memory re-rate.
