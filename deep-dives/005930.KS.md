## Core Thesis: The Only One-Stop Shop China Can Buy

Samsung is the memory oligopoly's broadest member — #1 DRAM (40%+ share), #1 NAND (29%, Counterpoint Q1 2026), #2 HBM and closing — and the only company on earth offering memory + foundry + packaging under one roof to Chinese AI builders. Micron is banned in China; CXMT is years behind on HBM. When ByteDance needs HBM plus foundry plus packaging from a single unrestricted supplier, there is exactly one option. ByteDance: $22B Samsung procurement, ~$55B/yr AI infra spend largely invisible to sell-side consensus.

**Memory is structural, not cyclical:** 3-5 year LTAs with quarterly supplier-floor pricing; DRAM $57K/kg and doubling QoQ (DDR4 $1.35→$16 in a year; DDR5 4x in a quarter); Apple absorbing a $400 MacBook price increase from memory pass-through; 2026 industry output FULLY SOLD with the supply-demand gap widening per Samsung's own Q1 commentary; shortage projected past 2028 (IDC: "potentially permanent strategic reallocation" of wafer capacity). Hyperscalers are offering to fund competitors' fabs — buyers paying for sellers' factories.

**Q1 2026 anchor:** $37.9B operating profit, +755% YoY — the strongest quarter in South Korean corporate history. ~95% from semiconductors, >90% memory-tied. DS division at 66% OP margin — NVIDIA-grade profitability from a memory company.

**Foundry renaissance (second engine):** AMD contracted at 2nm; Tesla AI5/AI6 confirmed; SiPh/CPO as a process option from H2 2026; NVIDIA and Google also multi-sourcing to Samsung due to structural TSMC capacity constraints (Nikkei Asia Jun 2026). AMD CPUs from 2028, NVIDIA on "some chip projects" — the world's two largest GPU/CPU designers choosing Samsung not on merit but because TSMC physically cannot serve everyone. P5 Fab 2 pulled forward six months inside a ₩120T capex program (600K wafers/month = doubles DRAM output). P5's multi-fab flexibility — HBM/DRAM/NAND/foundry on one campus — is optionality neither pure-memory SK Hynix nor pure-foundry TSMC possesses.

SK Hynix and Samsung are the ultimate "China must buy" positions, and Samsung alone adds the foundry and packaging Chinese AI builders also need. Every escalation step on the US/Taiwan controls timeline deepens this default-supplier position. Union strike risk: manageable, 7-12% OP impact (JPM).
