### Why CPO PIC Inspection Is Physics-Required

```
CPO module manufacturing flow:
  1. Fabricate PIC (photonic IC) on SiPh wafer         → TSEM
  2. Fabricate EIC (electronic IC)                      → TSMC
  3. Package PIC + EIC together (COUPE)                 → TSMC / 6451.TW
  4. INSPECT PIC for light leakage + waveguide defects  → MSScorps ← HERE
  5. Attach CW laser (external light source)            → SIVE
  6. Burn-in test completed module                      → AEHR

Step 4 is PHYSICS-REQUIRED because:
  - Light travels through waveguides etched into the PIC
  - Manufacturing defects cause light to leak from waveguides
  - Light leakage = signal loss = module performance degrades or fails
  - Infrared camera inspection is the ONLY non-destructive method to detect waveguide defects
  - MSScorps holds the KEY PATENT for this IR inspection method
  - Patent enforced in Taiwan, Japan, and US
  - Competitor (Enli) sued and NOT licensed — BLOCKED from market
```
