## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Ceramic materials, precision processing and internally developed production technology support Three-Circle across capacitors, optical components and energy materials. Higher AI power and optical connectivity increase component demand, while richer MLCC specifications can improve value per unit. Strong share in conventional ceramic ferrules does not imply a monopoly across every multi-fiber connector, optical package or entire data-center bill of materials.

Ceramic powders and precision tooling → MLCC/ceramic substrates/ferrules/membranes → electronics, connector and energy-system manufacturers → end-user deployment. Internal inputs are embedded in final-product cost. MT multi-fiber ferrules, conventional ceramic ferrules, crystal package bases and SOFC membranes are separate markets.
