## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics + DB4 Power (MOCVD Equipment — Layer -3)

## Core Thesis: The Near-ASML of Compound Semiconductors

AIXTRON holds **70-90% MOCVD market share** across most of its portfolio — the tool of record for growing compound-semiconductor epitaxial layers. Every InP laser in the AI supply chain (CW, DFB, EML, VCSEL) was grown inside an AIXTRON reactor; the G10-AsP is the instrument the entire photonics buildout runs through. Tier-0 confirmation (May 2026): asked about the InP shortage, the channel answer was direct — AIXTRON makes the equipment for InP manufacturing, and **LITE, COHR, and Sumitomo are all confirmed buying the machines.** The ASML parallel is structural, not promotional: a near-monopoly tool layer beneath an exploding device layer, at a fraction of the multiple — with the honest caveat that Veeco competes, so this is near-monopoly, not sole-source.

**The virtuous cycle (the mechanism):** InP shortage → EML shortage → SiPh demand up → CW laser demand up → InP fabs reallocate to higher-margin CW → more EML shortage → more SiPh. AIXTRON is the equipment layer enabling every loop of that cycle — all InP capacity expansion, whichever laser type wins, requires its reactors.

## TAM Expansion Lens

**Q1 (today):** InP epi for datacenter lasers — 65% of Q1 orders, accelerating. **Q2 (3-5yr):** GaN power for 800V DC datacenter (NVIDIA mandate), SiC for EV/grid, LiDAR, defense phased-array III-V, micro-LED. **Q3 (new):** photonic computing, quantum-dot qubits, 6G GaN.
