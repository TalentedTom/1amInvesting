## The Physics Monopoly

- **SiMaxx: 500 Wh/kg** — highest commercially available Li-ion on earth
- **SiCore: ~400 Wh/kg** — scalable, standard manufacturing compatible
- **SA128: 320 Wh/kg** — shipping NOW, L3Harris validated
- Conventional Li-ion: 250-300 Wh/kg maximum (graphite anode physics limit)
- The 300→500 Wh/kg gap is physics, not engineering. Graphite cannot get there. Silicon can.
