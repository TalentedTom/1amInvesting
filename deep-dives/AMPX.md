## Core Thesis: The Physics of Flight

Ford Principle: not "batteries" — the physics of flight and autonomous mobility. Without 450+ Wh/kg, eVTOL doesn't fly profitably, drones don't complete missions, robots run out of power.

- **SiMaxx: 500 Wh/kg** — highest commercially available Li-ion on earth
- **SiCore: ~400 Wh/kg** — scalable, standard-manufacturing compatible
- **SA128: 320 Wh/kg** — shipping NOW, L3Harris validated
- Conventional graphite-anode Li-ion tops out at 250-300 Wh/kg. The 300→500 gap is physics, not engineering.

**Dual moat (Axiom 14, the VICR parallel):** physics (no competitor within 2x of 500 Wh/kg) + regulatory (NDAA Section 164 bans Chinese battery components from US defense). Chinese batteries are blocked by law AND physics simultaneously.
