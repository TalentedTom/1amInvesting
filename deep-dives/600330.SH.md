## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (TFLN modulator substrate layer)

## Core Thesis: Domestic 8-Inch Lithium Niobate Monopoly for Next-Gen Optical Modulators

Tiantong is a diversified Chinese electronic materials company (magnetic materials, sapphire, pressure crystals, PV equipment) that is the only domestic producer mass-producing 8-inch optical-grade lithium niobate (LiNbO3) wafers. Globally, only 4 companies have this capability: Sumitomo, Shin-Etsu, Ferroperm (Denmark), and Tiantong. Thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) is emerging as the next-generation electro-optic modulator material for 1.6T/3.2T optical transceivers, where traditional silicon photonics hits bandwidth limits.

**LN wafer position (from 中航科技/同花顺/东方财富/新浪):**

6-inch yield >92%, 8-inch yield 70%+, 12-inch sample demonstrated. Current capacity 130-140万片/year. 420万片 expansion project (Phase 1 = 210万片 commissioned, Phase 2 delayed to 2029). Orders locked to Q4 2026. Cost 25-30% below Japanese competitors. LN wafer = 25-35% of optical modulator BOM cost. Customers: 中际旭创 (InnoLight, 800G module LN share rising 10→30%), 光库科技 (global #1 TFLN modulator maker), 新易盛.

**TFLN technology thesis:** At 1.6T, SiPh modulators are reaching bandwidth and power limits. TFLN offers higher electro-optic coefficient (r₃₃ ~30pm/V vs SiPh ~15pm/V at best), enabling >100GHz bandwidth at lower drive voltage. TFLN is positioned as "必选技术" (mandatory technology) for 3.2T and beyond. 2026 is considered TFLN "量产元年" (mass production year one). Tiantong partners with 青禾晶元 for Smart Cut + Bonding thin-film technology to produce LNOI (lithium niobate on insulator) wafers — the TFLN equivalent of SOI wafers.

**Rule 10 severe:** TFLN thin-film production line is "尚在调试中" (still being commissioned). Existing LN wafer business is real but volume was DECLINING in FY2025. The TFLN thesis is inside a conglomerate where magnetic materials (~40% of revenue), sapphire (~25%), and collapsing PV equipment (~8%) dominate.

**Rule 16d timing:** TFLN mass adoption is 24-36 months out (2028-2029 for 3.2T). Score capped at 65% WL by timing filter. At 57%, already below the cap.

## Cross-Book Read-Throughs

**DB1 supply chain position:** TFLN modulators would sit ALONGSIDE SiPh modulators in the optical chain, not replace them. At 800G/1.6T, SiPh (TSEM foundry, SOI.PA wafers) dominates. At 3.2T+, TFLN may complement or compete. If TFLN wins the 3.2T modulator battle:

**SOI.PA (watch):** TFLN modulators use LNOI wafers, not Photonics-SOI. A full transition to TFLN at 3.2T could reduce Soitec's Photonics-SOI TAM at the next speed tier. Currently immaterial — 3.2T is 2028-2029+. But worth monitoring as a long-term competitive risk to SOI's V1.

**TSEM (watch):** TSEM fabricates SiPh PICs for SiPh modulators. TFLN modulators use different fabrication (bulk LN crystal, bonding, not silicon foundry). Same competitive dynamic as SOI — if TFLN displaces SiPh at 3.2T, TSEM's photonics foundry TAM narrows. Again, immaterial near-term.

**光库科技 300620 (the investable TFLN expression):** Global #1 TFLN modulator maker, supplies NVIDIA/Google/Huawei. If TFLN wins, 光库 is the bottleneck — Tiantong supplies the wafers but 光库 makes the actual modulator chips. 光库 would be the more direct screen candidate (mid-stream vs upstream), though it faces the same A-share pricing issues.

**SIVE/LITE/COHR:** CW lasers work WITH both SiPh and TFLN modulators — the light source is modulator-agnostic. TFLN adoption doesn't threaten the laser chain. Net neutral.

## Key Risks (each scored)

1. **Governance (-2):** CSRC corrective order for 4 years of undisclosed related-party transactions. 5 executives warned. Controlling family selling 1.56% at the stock's all-time high while the company is losing money and warning about profitability uncertainty. This pattern (hide related-party dealings + sell at peak + warn about losses) is a governance red flag the framework cannot ignore.

2. **Rule 10 conglomerate (-1):** TFLN/LN is the growth thesis but it's inside a company where magnetic materials, sapphire, and collapsing PV equipment still dominate. The PV equipment division lost money in FY2025 (-62.4% revenue) and is exiting. Until PV is fully exited and TFLN ramps, the conglomerate drag persists.

3. **Execution (-1):** TFLN thin-film wafer production line "still being commissioned." Existing LN wafer volume declining in FY2025. Company lost money two consecutive periods. Analyst FY2026 profit estimate is ¥0.75亿 — barely breakeven. The gap between thesis and financials is extreme.

4. **Technology (-1):** SiPh modulators may solve the 3.2T bandwidth challenge through advanced modulation formats (PAM8, coherent), rendering TFLN unnecessary. SiPh has 20 years of manufacturing maturity and existing foundry infrastructure (TSEM, GFS). TFLN is unproven at commercial scale. If SiPh wins, Tiantong's growth thesis evaporates.
