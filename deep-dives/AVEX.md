## Supercycle: DB8 Defense (UAS / Combat Drones / Frontline Manufacturing)

## Core Thesis: A Time-Boxed Moat — ForgeX + Phoenix Ghost

The classification is deliberate: AVEX is a **temporal bottleneck** — a first-mover sole-source position with an expiry date, not a physics monopoly. Per the May 23 audit: **ForgeX, the in-theater additive-manufacturing capability, is first-mover in frontline manufacturing with $645.7M sole-sourced** — competitors can replicate the capability in an estimated 2-3 years, giving a 3-5 year monetization window. (Sole-source figure carried from the audit record; re-verification at the next filing is trigger #1.)

Beneath the window sits the volume franchise: **Phoenix Ghost, combat-proven in Ukraine since 2022**, inside a full stack — loitering munitions, autonomous UAS, unmanned surface vessels, AI-enabled ISR, C-UAS, and the ForgeX frontline manufacturing line. 10,200+ systems delivered or committed through end-2026.

The honest other half, unchanged: drones themselves are not a bottleneck. AeroVironment, Anduril, Kratos, L3Harris, Baykar, and dozens of startups build combat UAS; there is no physics barrier to replication. The Base of 52 prices exactly this — a real, revenue-generating edge that is *rented from time*, not owned by physics.
