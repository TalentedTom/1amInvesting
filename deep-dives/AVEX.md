## Why The Framework Scores AVEX Low

### 1. Not a Monopoly
Drones are NOT a bottleneck. Multiple companies make combat UAS:
- AeroVironment (Switchblade) — larger, proven
- Anduril (various autonomous systems) — better funded
- Kratos (Valkyrie) — jet-powered drones
- L3Harris — established defense prime
- Turkish Baykar (Bayraktar TB2) — export champion
- Dozens of startups

Phoenix Ghost is combat-proven, which matters. But it's one product in a crowded field with no physics barrier to replication.

### 2. PE Governance Risk
Madison Dearborn Partners retains 79.1% voting control. Public shareholders are minority owners with limited influence. The IPO is a liquidity event for the PE sponsor, not primarily a growth capital raise. This is a classic PE exit pattern — sell to public at peak narrative.

### 3. Hot IPO = Maximum Hype, Minimum Data
AVEX has been public for 7 DAYS. There is essentially zero public financial history to analyze. The $800M run rate is "preliminary." The $8.1B pipeline is "identified opportunities," not contracted backlog. The stock doubled in 2 sessions on momentum, not fundamentals.

### 4. Defense vs Supply Chain
The framework's DB8 Defense positions (EOS.AX at 74, PNG.V at 68) score higher because they have TECHNOLOGY MOATS:
- EOS: Speed-of-light directed energy weapon (physics barrier)
- PNG/Sonardyne: Gold standard underwater positioning (decades of qualification)
- AVEX: Loitering munition (many competitors can build similar)
