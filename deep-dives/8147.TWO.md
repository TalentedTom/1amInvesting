## Framework Concerns (Why Not Scored)

1. CONNECTOR MOAT IS UNPROVEN. TE Connectivity ($50B), Amphenol ($80B), Molex can all make high-speed connectors. No evidence Nextronics has a physics-based or patent-based moat. Being named in a Goldman note ≠ sole source.

2. DISAPPEARANCE TEST LIKELY FAILS. If Nextronics disappeared, NVIDIA could probably source from TE/Amphenol with 3-6 month requalification. Not a structural bottleneck.

3. 11 EMPLOYEES. Either a holding company, a design house outsourcing manufacturing, or the data is wrong. Cannot manufacture at NVIDIA scale with 11 people. Scalability uncertain.

4. 55x P/E ON $50M REVENUE. Already pricing significant growth. No margin of safety.

5. THERMAL CAGE = COMPETITIVE. Sheet metal + thermal design is not a physics barrier.
