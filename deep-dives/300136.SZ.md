## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Materials, antenna engineering and customer qualification let Sunway sell more component content into consumer devices, satellite terminals and future compute systems. Its integrated materials-to-module capability is useful, but RF components and MLCCs have alternative suppliers. It remains a beneficiary with execution-dependent platform expansion, rather than a physics chokepoint.

Material formulation → RF/thermal/passive component manufacturing → customer qualification → terminal/server/device installation. A satellite launch is not a Sunway terminal order. High-end MLCC demand expands with electronics complexity, but qualification, capacitance mix and yield decide supplier capture.
