## Core Thesis: Consumer RF Platform Converting to Satellite + Thermal + MLCC

信维 is transitioning from consumer-electronics RF to a multi-vertical platform: LEO satellite ground terminals (phased-array antenna, high-frequency connectors), chip thermal/TIM (AI chip cooling), automotive electronics, and high-end MLCC optionality (via 信维电科). ¥60亿 CSRC-approved private placement funding the three growth projects.

**Satellite:** North American satellite customer since 2021, second customer added, phased-array antenna in batch shipment. Best non-consumer vector.
**Thermal:** TIM1 + heat spreaders, batch supply to North American consumer customer, server samples in validation. Full-ramp project: ¥31.05亿 revenue, 31.44% IRR.
**MLCC:** Through 信维电科 (affiliate). High-cap parts passed domestic customer certification. North American server validation underway. NOT cleanly consolidated.

**FY2025:** Revenue ¥89.10亿 (+1.9%), NI ¥7.09亿 (+7.1%), GM 22.79% (+1.97pp). Q1 2026: Rev ¥19.92亿 (+14.3%), deducted NI +104%.

**Capacity warning:** 募投 order coverage only 9.93% of full annual capacity as of Q1 2026. The plan is large; the orders are still small.

**Rule 6/9:** FAIL chokepoint. Consumer RF, no physics monopoly. 📊 BENEFICIARY.
