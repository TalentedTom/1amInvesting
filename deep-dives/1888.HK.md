## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Kingboard combines laminates with glass yarn/fabric, copper foil and resin production. Integration protects supply and lets the company earn more of the materials spread when customers cannot obtain key inputs. AI boards and packaging need better electrical and thermal performance, raising content and specialty mix. Volume leadership and supply security support capture, but alternative qualified producers and cyclical capacity additions constrain sustained pricing power.

Glass yarn → woven electronic fabric; copper → treated foil; resin → prepreg and laminate bonding; these materials → copper-clad laminates → customers’ PCBs or package substrates. Kingboard earns its own external material and laminate sales. Selling a laminate to a fellow Kingboard Holdings subsidiary is an external sale for 1888’s subgroup, but that sibling’s finished-PCB revenue is not owned by 1888. Glass-core chip packages and system PCBs solve different functions; glass-core adoption does not automatically eliminate PCB laminate demand.
