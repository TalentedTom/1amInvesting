## Supercycle: AI PCB Materials (CCL upstream)

## Core Thesis: Global #1 CCL by Volume, Vertically Integrated, Riding the AI PCB Supercycle

Kingboard Laminates is the world's largest copper-clad laminate (CCL) producer by volume, with >200M sheets/year capacity and full vertical integration from electronic yarn → glass cloth → copper foil → CCL → PCB. CCL is the substrate material FROM WHICH all printed circuit boards are fabricated — every AI server, every smartphone, every car uses CCL-derived PCBs.

**The pricing supercycle (from 知乎/捷配/财联社/国泰海通):** 2025: 5 pricing rounds, cumulative +15-20%. 2026 YTD: 4+ rounds plus Jun Q3 notice, cumulative +40%+. 7628 electronic cloth: 6 rounds in 2026, ¥4.5→¥7.4/meter (+64%). FR-4 CCL: ¥150→¥240/sheet (+60%). Even in the June traditional off-season, pricing ACCELERATED (+¥0.7/m vs prior +¥0.5/m rounds) —淡季提价加速 means supply-demand gap is widening, not narrowing. Resonac (+30%), Mitsubishi Gas Chemical (+30%), and 台光電 all raising simultaneously.

**AI demand driver:** AI server PCB value ¥50K→¥700K per unit (14x traditional). CCL usage 3-5x per AI server vs standard. M6/M7/M8/M9 grade hierarchy: H100 = M7, GB200 = M8, 2027+ = M9. The grade upgrade PLUS volume growth = cubic demand compound. CCL cost is only 0.1-1.2% of AI equipment total investment — "不怕贵、就怕断" (don't fear expensive, fear disruption). Google, Amazon, NVIDIA are pre-locking capacity.

**Electronic cloth bottleneck (upstream of Kingboard):** Toyota weaving machines (the ONLY source for high-end electronic cloth looms) deliver globally 2,000-2,500/year vs industry need of 6,000-7,000 = 4,000 unit gap. ~30% of CCL manufacturers face work stoppages. This constrains the entire CCL chain for 2+ years. Kingboard's vertical integration (own cloth production) provides relative advantage — they have machines, competitors don't.

**FY2025 results (知乎/新浪):** Revenue HK$20.40B (+10%), GP HK$3.991B (+21.7%), GM 19.6% (+1.9pp), NI HK$2.442B (+83.6%), NM 12.0% (+4.8pp). EPS HK$0.782. Dividend HK$0.53 (25仙 final + 28仙 special). Net profit nearly doubled on only 10% revenue growth — operating leverage from pricing power on vertically integrated cost base.

### Why the Framework Scores at 65 WL — Not Higher

**Rule 9 FAIL:** If Kingboard disappeared, could AI PCBs still be made? Yes — 生益科技 (600183, China #2), Resonac (Japan), Mitsubishi Gas Chemical (Japan), 台光電 (Taiwan), 南亚新材 (688519), 金安国纪 (002636) all produce CCL. The market is competitive with multiple capable suppliers globally.

**Rule 6 FAIL:** Kingboard is the volume leader in FR-4 (standard grade) but TRAILS on high-end AI-grade CCL. M8/M9 products are led by Resonac, Mitsubishi Gas Chemical, and 台光電. Kingboard's M6 is entering customer validation, M8 is sampling — they are 1-2 years behind the high-end leaders. Global CR3 for high-end AI/automotive CCL exceeds 90% and doesn't include Kingboard.

**Cyclical, not structural:** CCL pricing power is shortage-driven, not moat-driven. When supply catches up (CCL expansion cycle 18-36 months), prices normalize. The framework classifies this as ⚡ BOTTLENECK (temporal), not 🔒 CHOKEPOINT.

## Cross-Book Read-Throughs

**PCB constraint validation:** Jabil Q2 transcript flagged "PCB constraints getting tighter." Kingboard's 7 pricing rounds confirm the upstream cause — CCL supply is THE bottleneck behind PCB constraints. This validates keeping EMC 2383.TW in the screen queue.

**Dongshan Precision (002384.SZ):** Dongshan's PCB segment (64% of revenue) buys CCL from Kingboard and competitors. CCL pricing +40% directly compresses Dongshan's PCB margins unless passed through. Cross-pressure on Dongshan's non-optical business.

**Fenghua / MLCC chain:** CCL pricing pressure flows through to PCB pricing → flows through to server assembly costs → but remains immaterial vs GPU/memory costs. AI equipment pricing is GPU-constrained, not CCL-constrained. No impact on MLCC chain.

**LPK.DE:** Glass core substrates (CoPoS) use different materials than CCL — glass + ABF, not fiberglass + copper + resin. CCL shortage does NOT affect the glass substrate pathway. If anything, CCL shortages make the glass alternative MORE attractive as a substitute.

## Key Risks (each scored)

1. **Competitive (-1):** Multiple global competitors across all CCL grades. At the high end (M8/M9), Kingboard is BEHIND Resonac, Mitsubishi Gas Chemical, and 台光電. Volume leadership in FR-4 doesn't translate to technology leadership in AI-grade materials.

2. **Cyclicality (-1):** CCL is a commodity chemical product. The 2022-2023 down-cycle saw FR-4 prices crash ~30%, margins collapse, and Kingboard NI fall to HK$1.33B from HK$4.9B peak (2021). Current pricing is shortage-driven; when 18-36 month expansion cycles deliver new capacity, pricing normalizes. The framework scores the CURRENT cycle's peak, which makes the ceiling generous by design.

3. **High-end qualification gap (-1):** AI servers are moving from M7→M8→M9 grade CCL. Kingboard's M6 is in qualification, M8 is sampling, M9 is pre-research. If the market shifts to M9 by 2027-2028 (as 224G SerDes demands), Kingboard may be locked out of the highest-value segment.

## Re-Rate Triggers

1. **M6 qualification at head PCB factory (2026):** If successful, proves Kingboard can compete above FR-4. High-end share rising to ≥30% of revenue validates the transition. COUNTER: qualification failure.

2. **NVIDIA/Huawei supply chain certification:** If either certifies Kingboard for M8+ CCL, the high-end gap closes. COUNTER: Japanese competitors retain exclusive positioning.

3. **Q3-Q4 pricing sustainability:** If +10-15% Q3 round sticks AND Q4 round follows, cumulative FY2026 pricing approaches +50-60%. The margin model revises upward. COUNTER: PCB customers resist, demand softens.

4. **Electronic cloth supply normalization (2027+):** When Toyota weaving machine deliveries catch up, electronic cloth prices normalize → CCL cost advantage from self-supply diminishes. COUNTER: demand grows faster than cloth supply additions.

5. **CYCLICALITY FALSIFICATION:** If CCL pricing holds above ¥200/sheet through 2027 (vs the model's reversion toward ¥180-200), the "cyclical" classification weakens and the multiple deserves re-examination. Currently no evidence to override the cyclical assumption.
