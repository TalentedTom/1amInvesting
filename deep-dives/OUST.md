## Core Thesis: The Digital-LiDAR Monopoly, Now With Color

Ouster is the only company building **digital LiDAR** while every competitor committed to analog — and the moat *widens* each generation because digital is software-defined and silicon-scaled while analog carries mechanical wear parts. **REV8 (May 4, 2026) is the architectural-disruption event: the world's first native color LiDAR** — depth AND color in one sensor, no camera needed. The L4 custom chip doubles range and resolution (10.4M points/sec, 200m detection), is auto-grade qualified, and Google (Waymo) + Volvo confirmed adoption with samples shipping July 2026.

The disruption is structural: before REV8, autonomy needed LiDAR (3D geometry) + camera (2D color) + fusion software — two sensors and merge logic, with cost, complexity, and failure points. After REV8, one sensor carries 3D geometry *with* color, eliminating the camera for many applications. No competitor has this, and none can add it without a full redesign (Luminar/Hesai/RoboSense are all analog). **The Excel classification is the correct one: a physical-AI perception primitive — every robot, drone, and AV needs vision+depth, and this is the only digital+color sensor that delivers both.** The SIVE parallel: the entire field committed to the wrong architecture.

**NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion qualification (May 2026):** REV8 qualified for the Hyperion L4 platform — every OEM building on Hyperion uses the sensor by default. Google + Volvo + NVIDIA is the autonomy trifecta, the same platform-lock pattern as ShunSin in NVIDIA's CPO chain. Hyperion is a *current* platform (production vehicles 18-24 months), which compresses the timing discount.
