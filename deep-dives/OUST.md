# 2. Catalyst stack

## 2.1 AIM Intelligent Machines

The AIM agreement is likely the biggest near-term spark.

Ouster announced a strategic agreement with AIM Intelligent Machines to supply digital lidar for AI-powered autonomous heavy earthmoving equipment used in mining, construction, and defense sites globally.

This matters because heavy machinery has clear ROI:

- fewer accidents
- less labor dependency
- improved productivity
- autonomous operation in dust, darkness, and remote sites
- relevance to defense, construction, and mining

This is better than waiting for one passenger-car OEM to adopt lidar years from now.

## 2.2 Benchmark high-volume Rev8 manufacturing

Ouster expanded its long-term manufacturing partnership with Benchmark to support high-volume production of the Rev8 OS sensor family.

The release points to:

- complex microelectronics assembly
- automated optical assembly
- established capacity above 100,000 units per year
- planned 10-year production life for Rev8 sensors

This changes the question from:

> Can Ouster build a better sensor?

to:

> Can Ouster ship Rev8 in volume?

The manufacturing answer is now stronger.

## 2.3 Rev8 BlueCity + BABA compliance

Ouster announced that Rev8 complies with Build America, Buy America requirements, making Rev8 eligible for federally funded infrastructure deployments.

This matters for:

- state and local transportation agencies
- engineering firms
- smart infrastructure
- intelligent transportation
- smart cities
- transit
- tolling

BlueCity with Rev8 provides:

- 500-foot advanced detection
- optional native color
- AI traffic-management software
- potential replacement of multiple cameras, radars, and inductive loops

BlueCity already has 700+ contracted site deployments, including the three largest US deployments to date.

## 2.4 NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion

Ouster’s Rev8 OS family is qualified for NVIDIA DRIVE Hyperion for level 4 autonomous-vehicle development.

This does not mean NVIDIA is buying OUST in large volume immediately.

But it validates Rev8 inside a major autonomy development ecosystem.

## 2.5 ARGUS counter-UAS / defense

Ouster announced a strategic agreement with ARGUS Interception for net-based counter-drone systems.

This is not yet material revenue proof, but it strengthens Ouster’s positioning in defense and critical infrastructure.

# 10. Current-price implication

At roughly **$58/share**, Ouster is already pricing a lot.

Approximate market cap:

> $3.59B

At the framework’s 20x OP multiple, the market cap implies required operating profit of:

> $3.59B / 20 = $179M OP

The FY2028 model gives:

> $90M OP

So the current stock price is asking for roughly:

> 2.0x the FY2028 operating-profit model

At a 47% gross margin and about $200M opex, Ouster would need roughly:

> ($179M OP + $200M opex) / 47% GM = ~$806M FY2028 revenue

The FY2028 revenue model is:

> $620M

So the stock is not crazy if 2028 revenue can reach $800M+ and operating leverage arrives fast. But that is not the base case yet.

# 12. Main risks

## 12.1 Partnerships are not disclosed revenue

AIM is strategic and high-volume, but no dollar amount was disclosed.

ARGUS is strategic, but no dollar amount was disclosed.

Benchmark is capacity, not demand.

The stock is pricing conversion before conversion.

## 12.2 Software revenue opacity

BlueCity / Gemini are real, but Ouster is still mostly a product-revenue company.

Until software revenue is disclosed clearly, OUST should not be modeled like a SaaS company.

## 12.3 Gross margin must hold during volume ramp

Q1 GAAP gross margin was 43%.

That is good for hardware, but not enough to justify a massive platform multiple unless revenue and software attach scale.

## 12.4 Chinese competition

Hesai and RoboSense can pressure cost in many commercial lidar categories.

Ouster’s defense:

- Western supply chain
- digital architecture
- Rev8 native color
- BABA compliance
- software
- infrastructure / defense customers

## 12.5 Not a true Rule 6 chokepoint

Robots and machines need perception.

They do not necessarily need Ouster.

Some systems will use:

- cameras
- radar
- stereo cameras
- Hesai lidar
- RoboSense lidar
- Aeva FMCW lidar
- Mobileye-style stacks
- customer-built sensor fusion
