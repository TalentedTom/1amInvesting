## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

The recurring demand unit is a processed wafer multiplied by material consumed per process step. More fabs increase addressable throughput, while additional deposition steps increase material intensity. Qualification creates switching costs, but it does not establish sole-source status. DB6 memory and DB12 materials demand apply. LNG and other chemical operations diversify the group and dilute pure semiconductor exposure.

AI training, inference and memory capacity → DRAM/NAND/logic wafer producers → deposition, lithography and fab-delivery systems → Yoke qualified precursor, resist, gas and service content. Qualification and process compatibility are the immediate barriers. If Yoke disappeared, alternative chemistries and vendors could eventually serve these processes; the near-term substitution cost supports BOTTLENECK classification. LNG insulation and services have different customers and demand cycles, diluting a pure semiconductor classification.
