## Supercycle: DB1 CPO Photonics (upstream) + DB11 Materials Escalation

## Core Thesis: One of Two Dominant InP Substrate Suppliers, and the Only One Selling Externally at Scale

AXT manufactures compound semiconductor substrates — InP, GaAs, germanium — plus raw-material JVs. AI relevance: InP substrates sit upstream of every high-speed optical chip, EML/CW laser, and eventual CPO/optical-I/O device. Reuters: AXT ~35% global InP substrate share, Sumitomo ~40%, JX ~10% — and Sumitomo consumes most of its output internally, making the *externally available* market far more AXT-dependent than raw share suggests.

The accelerant is DB11 policy tightness: China began throttling InP export licenses after February 2025; AXT calls export permits its most significant challenge; average 6-inch InP wafer price rose ~250% to ~$5,000 post-controls. The structure cuts both ways — AXT is US-listed (Fremont) but manufacturing is China-heavy (AXT-Tongmei, Beijing; partial ownership in ten Chinese raw-material companies). Scarcity beneficiary and export-permit hostage simultaneously.

## The Coherent Deal (Jun 25, 2026 — 8-K, filing-grade)

Three-year Master Development and Supply Agreement, AXT-Tongmei × Coherent, 6-inch InP wafer substrates. **Prepayment $22,288,500** against committed capacity; AXT commits to Beijing capacity increases 2026-2028. First confirmed prepayment at this name — the framework's strongest pricing-power signal, now at the substrate layer. The teeth cut both ways: unused prepayment refundable; **Coherent may terminate if AXT misses the capacity commitment >6 successive months**; AXT must offer additional capacity to Coherent on same terms. A prepayment that creates an obligation. Full agreement files with the Q2 10-Q — economics disclosure is a live trigger.

## Q1 2026 Baseline (first P&L proof of ramp)

Revenue $26.9M (Q4'25 $23.0M, Q1'25 $19.4M); GAAP GM 29.6% / non-GAAP 29.9%; GAAP net loss narrowed to $1.6M ($0.03). Mix: InP $13.6M, GaAs $5.4M, Ge $0.2M, raw-material JVs $7.6M. Top-5 customers 32%, none >10%. April raise **$632.5M gross** for Tongmei InP expansion + 6-inch R&D. Management: InP backlog >$100M; Q2 revenue up to ~$34M recognizable under current permits/non-permit shipments; Q2 InP >$17M; Q2 return to profitability.

## Capacity Roadmap (the heart of the thesis)

Ahead of plan to **double InP capacity in 2026** → ~$35M/quarter InP capability by year-end 2026. Next: another doubling in 2027 (new Beijing facility) → ~$65-70M/quarter. 2028 expansion planning underway. If the market moves 3-/4-inch → 6-inch InP for AI optics and CPO, AXT holds one of the few near-term paths to qualified capacity. Timing discipline: Q1 InP was $13.6M — the ceiling models conversion, not end-state capacity.

4. China export-control regime relaxes before capacity converts → scarcity-margin model falls
