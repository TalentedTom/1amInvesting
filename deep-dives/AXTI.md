## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (InP/GaAs Substrate — Layer -2, Deepest Material)

## Core Thesis: The Raw Crystal Underneath Everything

AXT grows the indium phosphide and gallium arsenide substrate crystals that EVERYTHING in the photonics supply chain is built on. Every InP laser, every InP photodetector, every SiPh chip using III-V integration — they all start as an AXT substrate wafer. InP backlog hit a record >$60M. Capacity doubling by end-2026. Broadening from niche customers to Tier-1 laser manufacturers and transceiver makers. Two demand vectors: scale-out optics (doubling 2026 and 2027) and scale-up/CPO (inflection 2028-2029).

## Supply Chain Position — Layer -2

```
AXT grows InP/GaAs substrate crystals ← DEEPEST LAYER (-2)
  └── IQE grows epitaxial layers on AXT substrates (Layer -1.5)
      └── SOI.PA provides Photonics-SOI substrates (Layer -1)
          └── TSEM fabricates SiPh chips (Layer 0)
              └── SIVE provides CW lasers (Layer 0)
                  └── AAOI/COHR/LITE assemble transceivers (Layer 1)
```

AXT is the ORIGIN POINT. The raw crystal. Framework Axiom 3: "The deeper in the stack, the stronger the moat."

## The China Export Risk (Framework-Critical)

AXT manufactures in China. InP substrates require Chinese government export permits. This creates a BINARY risk:

**Permits flow:** Revenue surges (Q3 2025: InP +250% QoQ). Backlog converts. Growth accelerates.

**Permits delayed:** Revenue collapses (Q4 2025: missed guidance). Backlog sits unconverted. Stock drops.

The Q4 2025 revenue miss ($23M vs $28M Q3) was ENTIRELY caused by slower-than-expected export permits. Not demand. Not technology. PERMITS. This is geopolitical risk that the framework can't model — it's a binary variable controlled by the Chinese Ministry of Commerce.

CEO Young says permits improved in early 2026, but this risk is PERMANENT as long as manufacturing is in China. The $550M capital raise could partially fund non-China capacity, but that's years away.
