## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (Substrate Layer — Underneath Everything)

## The >95% Monopoly

Every silicon photonics chip is fabricated on a Photonics-SOI substrate. Soitec holds **>95% market share** (Bank of America, March 2026).

- **Smart Cut technology:** 30 years of refinement. 4,300 active patents. Hydrogen ion implantation + thermal cleave.
- **IP licensing model:** Makes money on 100% of SOI wafers — either sells directly OR collects licensing fees from competitors using Smart Cut. (Framework Rule 3: IP-licensing exempt from pricing culture.)
- **220nm silicon layer on 2µm buried oxide, thickness uniformity within 1nm across entire wafer.** This precision is non-trivial to replicate.
- **"There is no second source."** — KawzInvests (Apr 2026). TSMC, GlobalFoundries, STMicro, and Intel ALL use Soitec's Photonics-SOI. Four foundries, one substrate supplier.

"Every photonics chip Tower ships maps to a Soitec wafer order." — Vantix Research

## The 4x CPO Content Step-Up (Critical TAM Multiplier)

**SOURCE:** KawzInvests, April 14, 2026:
```
Pluggable transceiver: ~50mm² of Photonics-SOI per device
CPO engine:            ~200mm² of Photonics-SOI per device
Content step-up:       4x per device

Same monopoly. Same company. 4x the revenue per unit.
```

This means the transition from pluggable to CPO doesn't just maintain Soitec's revenue — it MULTIPLIES it by 4x per device, even before unit volume growth is considered.

**Demand math with content step-up:**
```
Pluggable era (peak ~84M units):
  84M × 50mm² = 4.2B mm² of Photonics-SOI demand

CPO era (overlapping with pluggable, 2028-2030):
  Even at 10M CPO engines: 10M × 200mm² = 2.0B mm² (half of peak pluggable)
  At 30M CPO engines: 30M × 200mm² = 6.0B mm² (1.4x peak pluggable)
  
  PLUS pluggable doesn't disappear — both S-curves overlap
  Total demand: pluggable + CPO simultaneously
  
CPO TAM: $15B by 2030 at 263% CAGR (KawzInvests citing LightCounting)
```

The 4x content step-up means Soitec's Photonics-SOI revenue trajectory is NOT linear growth — it's a step function when CPO ramps. €100M today at 50mm²/device becomes €400M+ at 200mm²/device on the SAME number of devices. Unit growth on top of that = exponential.

## Multi-Platform Optionality

Soitec isn't just Photonics-SOI. It's a platform company with 5 substrate technologies:

| Platform | Market | Status |
|----------|--------|--------|
| **Photonics-SOI** | AI datacenter SiPh | **>95% monopoly, growing 100%+ YoY** |
| RF-SOI | Smartphone RF front-end | Recovering (channel clearing) |
| POI (Piezoelectric) | 5G filters | Skyworks deal, growing content/phone |
| FD-SOI | Edge AI, automotive | Growing AI adoption |
| SmartSiC | EV power electronics | Delayed, €41M write-down |
| **LNOI** | 1.6T-3.2T modulators | **Future — where SiPh alone can't keep up** |
| **GaN-on-Si** | Power electronics | Milestone: >50% efficiency with NTU |

LNOI is the sleeper. For 1.6T-3.2T speeds where silicon photonics modulators struggle, Lithium Niobate on Insulator becomes the preferred substrate. Soitec can supply BOTH Photonics-SOI AND LNOI from the same technology base — covering the full speed spectrum.
