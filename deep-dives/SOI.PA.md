## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics — Layer -1, the Substrate Beneath Everything

## Core Thesis: The >95% Monopoly

Every silicon photonics chip is fabricated on a Photonics-SOI substrate, and Soitec holds **>95% share** (BofA, Mar 2026). Smart Cut: 30 years of refinement, **3,500+ active patents**, hydrogen-implant + thermal cleave delivering a 220nm silicon layer on 2µm buried oxide with **1nm thickness uniformity across a 300mm wafer** — an order of magnitude beyond RF-SOI requirements. The IP-licensing model means Soitec earns on 100% of SOI wafers — sold directly or licensed (Rule 3 exempt from pricing culture). TSMC, GlobalFoundries, Tower, STMicro, and Samsung ALL fabricate on Soitec substrate: five foundries, one supplier, **no second source** at 300mm volume. PhotonCap (PhD, Apr 2026): "no competitor with meaningful Photonics-SOI volume identified."

Framework Axiom 3: the deeper in the stack, the stronger the moat. Soitec sits beneath the foundries themselves.

## SiN Threat — DISMISSED (hardcoded intelligence, PhotonCap Apr 2026)

SiN is a **complement, not a replacement**: (1) passive — cannot modulate at datacenter GHz speeds (thermal kHz, piezo-assisted hundreds of MHz); (2) every hybrid platform (Tower PH18, GFS Fotonix, IMEC iSiPP) is SOI-based with SiN layered ON TOP; (3) stacking SiN **raises** the uniformity bar for the SOI underneath. Hybrid platforms make Soitec MORE critical. No risk deduction.

## The Content Step-Up (structural TAM multiplier)

KawzInvests (Apr 2026): pluggable transceiver ~50mm² of Photonics-SOI; CPO engine ~200mm² — **4x per device**, before unit growth. Escalation path: 1.6T pluggable ~75-100mm²; 3.2T CPO ~300-400mm² — potentially 8x+ vs the 800G base. Each speed grade consumes more SOI real estate; the architecture transition multiplies the monopoly's revenue per unit. Demand math: peak pluggable (~84M units × 50mm²) ≈ 4.2B mm²; 30M CPO engines alone ≈ 6.0B mm² — and the S-curves overlap rather than substitute.
