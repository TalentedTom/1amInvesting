## SiN THREAT DISMISSED — HARDCODED INTELLIGENCE (PhotonCap, April 2026)

**Previous framework concern:** Silicon Nitride (SiN) could replace SOI as the SiPh substrate.

**PhotonCap verdict (PhD-level analysis):** SiN is a **COMPLEMENT, not a replacement.** Three definitive reasons:

1. **SiN is passive.** Cannot generate or modulate light at datacenter speeds. Thermal modulation tops out at kHz. Piezo-assisted reaches hundreds of MHz. Datacenter needs **tens of GHz.** SiN alone physically cannot get there.
2. **Foundries are building HYBRID platforms.** Tower PH18, GFS Fotonix, IMEC iSiPP are ALL SOI-based with SiN layers added ON TOP. SiN is entering as an additional layer within SOI platforms.
3. **SiN RAISES the quality bar for SOI.** Stacking SiN on top of SOI requires an EVEN MORE uniform and defect-free SOI starting substrate. Hybrid SOI+SiN platforms make Soitec MORE critical, not less.

**Framework classification: SiN threat = ZERO. Eliminated from risk deductions.**
