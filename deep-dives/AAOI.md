## Core Thesis

AAOI is the U.S.-anchored, vertically integrated 800G / 1.6T pluggable transceiver ramp name.

The thesis is not that AAOI is a permanent monopoly. It is a temporal bottleneck: demand exceeds qualified supply, AAOI is expanding U.S. module and laser capacity, and the company can convert capacity into revenue before the standardized module layer normalizes.

Key standing facts from the prior artifact:

- Q1 2026 revenue: $151.1M.
- FY2026 guidance: >$1.1B revenue and >$140M non-GAAP operating income.
- Customer demand exceeded capacity through mid-2027 in the prior model.
- Management capacity/ramp targets imply a multi-billion-dollar annualized run-rate by mid/late 2027.
- AAOI is vertically integrated in key laser supply, but still exposed to module ASP/margin normalization.

## What v5.0 Reveals

The old annual model made AAOI look like a FY2028 story.

The quarterly model shows the stock-moving window starts earlier:

- **Q3 2026:** near-term forward-4Q ceiling is about spot price.
- **Q4 2026:** ceiling steps to ~$210 if Q4 capacity conversion is credible.
- **Q1 2027:** ceiling steps to ~$296 as Q4 2027 begins entering the forward window.
- **Q2 2027:** the key front-run quarter; ceiling steps to ~$374.
- **Q3-Q4 2027:** FY2028 run-rate becomes visible; ceiling reaches ~$434-$466.
- **Q1-Q2 2029:** peak pluggable cycle reaches ~$582.

This is the framework edge: do not wait for FY2028 revenue to appear. Track when the forward 4Q earnings window begins to include the ramp.

## Risks

1. **Module ASP pressure:** standardized 800G and eventually 1.6T can become procurement categories.
2. **Margin durability:** the 33% OP path requires scale opex leverage and 1.6T / ELSFP mix to offset price-downs.
3. **Customer concentration:** large hyperscaler dependence can create shipment timing volatility.
4. **Capacity conversion:** management capacity is not revenue; qualification, utilization, yield, and customer allocation matter.
5. **CPO/NPO cannibalization:** the pluggable S-curve likely peaks before 2030.
