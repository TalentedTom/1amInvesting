## Core Thesis: The Infrastructure Layer Underneath China's WFE Boom

正帆 sits between fabs and tools: high-purity gas/chemical delivery systems, Gas Box/Chemical Box/Bubbler modules for WFE tools, quartz/SiC consumables (via 汉京半导体 acquisition), specialty gases, precursors, and MRO services. Every tool OEM in the quintet (NAURA/AMEC/Piotech/ACMR/Hwatsing) needs 正帆-type infrastructure.

**Business-quality transition:** Non-equipment/OPEX share rose from 31% to 42.3% of revenue, and from 44.8% to 51.6% of new contracts. Shifting from one-time project CAPEX toward recurring OPEX (gases, materials, MRO, consumables).

**汉京 acquisition:** China's first ultra-high-purity quartz line for sub-10nm, first semiconductor-grade SiC component line. Customers include TEL, Hitachi Kokusai, TSMC, NAURA, Piotech, AMEC. High-upside but 2025 performance weak.

**FY2025 trough:** Revenue ¥49.16亿 (-10.1%), OP ¥0.93亿 (from ¥5.95亿 in FY2024). Process systems ¥28.36亿 (GM 20.87%), Equipment components ¥8.56亿 (+22%, GM 25.94%), Gas/materials ¥6.79亿 (+25%, GM 12.10%), MRO ¥5.44亿 (+20%, GM 24.70%).
**Q1 2026:** Revenue ¥9.70亿 (+43.4%). New orders ¥12亿 (+58.6%). Semiconductor orders ¥7.05亿 (+93.6%). NI still negative (-¥2,560万).
