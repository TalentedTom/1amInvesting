## Supercycle: Optical Test & Measurement

## What VIAVI Does

VIAVI makes test, monitoring, and measurement equipment for telecommunications networks, data centers, and optical systems. Two segments:

**NSE (Network & Service Enablement) — 79% of revenue:**
- Fiber test instruments (field & lab)
- Optical transport test (400G/800G/1.6T)
- Protocol test (Ethernet, PCIe)
- Aerospace & defense (PNT, timing)
- Spirent acquisition (high-speed Ethernet, network security test)
- CPO production test (spectral performance measurement)

**OSP (Optical Security & Performance) — 21% of revenue:**
- Anti-counterfeiting pigments (currency, IDs)
- 3D sensing filters (facial recognition)
- Automotive coatings

## The CPO Production Test Angle

CEO Khaykin on CPO:

"We are seeing a lot of momentum on this whole co-packaged uptick area, and that plays extremely well to the traditional Viavi strength with the old JDS Uniphase products that go into the production line where you're measuring spectral performance, the optical performance of all the various optics. We are selling now a lot of that equipment to the semiconductor vendors as they develop their integrated packaged optic solutions."

VIAV is selling optical production test tools to SEMICONDUCTOR vendors (TSMC, Intel, Samsung) for CPO development. The JDS Uniphase heritage in optical measurement gives VIAV domain expertise that some competitors lack.

HOWEVER: this is expertise, not monopoly. Keysight at $31B MC has its own optical test solutions. Anritsu and Rohde & Schwarz compete.

## The "If Disappeared" Test — FAILS

If VIAV disappeared: Keysight absorbs most datacenter/optical test demand. Anritsu captures Asian market. EXFO takes fiber test share. Rohde & Schwarz serves A&D test. No architecture breaks. Test is a COMPETITIVE market with 6+ credible alternatives.
