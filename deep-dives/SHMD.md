## Supercycle: DB5 AI Chip Packaging Materials (equipment layer) + DB1-adjacent (glass-core/CoPoS)

## The Screening Question, Answered

The dive existed to answer one question from the Kuo CoPoS breadcrumb: the 510×515mm glass core has two hard problems — TGV formation (LPK owns it via LIDE) and copper fill/metallization (unowned in the book). **Does SCHMID own the metallization step?**

**Answer: partially — it owns the LINE, not the CHEMISTRY.** SCHMID runs the only full-TGV lab capable of every step from bare glass to finished advanced IC package (etch, metallize, both-side RDL — 3,000 sqm, Freudenstadt, positioned as sole supplier of the complete flow), has shipped its first InfinityLine P+ panel plating system and C+ system (to a leading Japanese advanced-packaging customer, Dec 2025), launched Any Layer ET (full panel-level damascene: DRIE+PVD+ECD+CMP, organic/hybrid/glass compatible, process-license + equipment model), and its acidic TGV etch covers 100-600mm panels — spanning the CoPoS 510×515 MP spec. **But the hard-problem chemistry — making copper adhere to glass — belongs to Atotech (MKS): VitroCoat GI + CupraTech GI electroless copper is the industry's leading solution.** And the industry gate is unpassed by anyone: LIDE drills 1:50 aspect-ratio vias while metallization is publicly demonstrated only at 5:1, and **no company has announced CpK 1.33 on metallization at panel scale — the number that unlocks the entire glass ramp.** SCHMID is the integrator of a flow whose hardest step it equips but does not solve, in a market that has not yet reached HVM.
