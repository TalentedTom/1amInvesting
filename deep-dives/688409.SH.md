## Core Thesis: The Parts Layer Behind China's WFE Boom

Every China WFE OEM ramp needs precision parts. NAURA, AMEC, Piotech, ACMR, Hwatsing — all need chambers, liners, gas distribution plates, heater plates, valves, gas boxes, UHP gas lines, coatings, modules. 富创 is one of China's broadest domestic WFE subsystem suppliers.

**NOT a tool OEM** — a subsystem/parts supplier. Lower margins, higher customer concentration, but structurally tied to the same CXMT/YMTC/SMIC capex cycle. Parts are ~50-55% of WFE equipment value.

**FY2025 trough:** Revenue ¥35.43亿 (+16.6%), NI -¥861万 (loss). Main GM 22.45%. Beijing/surface-treatment capacity utilization <30%. Forward investment in capacity/talent/R&D ahead of demand.
**Q1 2026 recovery:** Revenue ¥10.43亿 (+36.8%), NI turned positive (¥5,794万).

**Rule 6/9:** FAIL global (UCT/Ichor/VAT/MKS exist). PASS China domestic subsystem bottleneck. Classification: 📊 BENEFICIARY (lower moat than tool OEMs).

## Cross-Book: The WFE subsystem layer

Every China WFE OEM in the quintet (NAURA/AMEC/Piotech/ACMR/Hwatsing) needs 富创's parts. This is the supplier-behind-the-supplier: fabs buy tools from OEMs → OEMs buy parts from 富创. Peer/overlap with KFMI (targets/ESC) and different from gas suppliers (正帆科技).

**Upgrade trigger:** GM recovery above 28-30% for two quarters. Without margin proof, revenue growth alone doesn't justify the valuation.
