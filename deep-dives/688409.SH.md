## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Fortune supplies the qualified parts behind domestic and international semiconductor tools. Complex surfaces, welding, cleanliness and customer co-development support retention. Higher domestic tool output, more demanding process steps and local sourcing expand demand. It remains a supplier with alternatives rather than a global sole-source platform, and its factory utilization must improve enough to absorb substantial depreciation.

Fab capex → tool OEM procurement → qualified parts and subsystems → finished process tools. Fortune captures only its supplied content. NAURA, AMEC, Piotech, ACM and Hwatsing are relevant domestic equipment ecosystems, but undisclosed customer identities and exact annual purchase shares are not invented. UCT, Ichor, VAT and other component vendors show that this is competitive supply.
