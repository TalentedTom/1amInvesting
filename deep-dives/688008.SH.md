## Core Thesis: China's Memory-Wall Toll Collector

澜起 collects tolls on the memory wall: DDR5 RCD, MRCD/MDB, CKD, PCIe Retimer, CXL MXC — every chip that sits BETWEEN the CPU/GPU and DRAM. World's largest memory-interconnect chip supplier by revenue (36.8% global share, 2024). JEDEC standard-setting role for RCD, MDB, CKD. This is the China-localized version of the MRVL/ALAB/RMBS CXL thesis.

**Why inference changes everything:** AI training was GPU-heavy. Inference and agents make systems CPU-heavy and memory-heavy. More CPUs = more memory modules = more 澜起 content per server. Company said it directly: "as AI workloads move toward inference and Agent, CPU importance rises, more memory modules needed, expanding demand for memory-interface, PCIe, and CXL chips."

**FY2025:** Revenue ¥54.56亿 (+49.94%), interconnect ¥51.39亿 (+53.43%, GM 65.57%), NI ¥22.36亿. Q1 2026: Rev ¥14.61亿 (+19.5%), interconnect GM 71.5%, new-product revenue ¥2.69亿 (19% of interconnect, +93.8% YoY). New products = MRCD/MDB, PCIe Retimer, CKD, CXL MXC.

**Rule 6/9:** FAIL global chokepoint (Rambus, Renesas, Astera, Marvell exist). PASS China domestic memory-interface bottleneck + global DDR5 RCD leader. Classification: ⚡ BOTTLENECK.
