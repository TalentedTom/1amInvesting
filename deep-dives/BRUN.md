## Supercycle: Watchlist — Neocloud AI Infrastructure

## Core Thesis: Contracted Neocloud at SPAC Pricing

Boost Run is a neocloud AI infrastructure provider — GPU compute, CPU nodes, managed Kubernetes, shared storage — listed via SPAC merger on Nasdaq May 11, 2026. NVIDIA Preferred Cloud Partner with Exemplar Cloud status on Blackwell architecture (exclusive group of validated providers).

The thesis: $1.41B non-cancelable contracted backlog on a $2.48B market cap, priced at 6.7x FY2028 P/E with 27% net margins. Either the market is wrong (discovery opportunity) or there's SPAC-origin execution risk the forward numbers don't capture.

NOT a bottleneck/chokepoint — this is a capacity provider, not a technology supplier. Moat is contracts + NVIDIA validation + compliance certifications, not physics or patents. Included as Watchlist, not core portfolio.

## Key Risks

1. **No technology moat.** Cloud capacity is replicable. Contracts are the moat, not physics. When contracts expire, customers can move.
2. **SPAC origin.** Warrant dilution, institutional skepticism, limited operating history as public company.
3. **Customer concentration.** Thinking Machines Lab = $472M = 33% of total backlog. Single-customer risk.
4. **Execution on 10x ramp.** $27M → $265M in one year is aggressive. Revenue recognized only as capacity deploys — deployment delays = revenue delays.
5. **Dell dependency.** $1.44B purchase agreement = reliance on single hardware supplier.

## Why Watchlist (not core portfolio)

BRUN doesn't fit the Adaptive Bottleneck & Scalability Framework — it's a consumer of AI infrastructure, not a supplier of irreplaceable components. The moat is contracts and certifications, not physics or patents. Included for monitoring because the valuation (6.7x FY2028 P/E, 57% of MC in contracted backlog) is compelling enough to watch for entry if the thesis proves out over the next 1-2 quarters of execution.

Watch for: Q2-Q3 2026 revenue vs $265M model (first verification of contracted → recognized), additional contract announcements, warrant dilution schedule, customer diversification beyond TML.
