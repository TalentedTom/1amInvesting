## Supercycle: DB1 CPO Photonics (physical optical plumbing) + AI datacenter interconnect

## Core Thesis: Integrated Fiber Leader with HCF Technical Seat and AI Interconnect Option

YOFC is an integrated optical communications company: preforms, fibers, cables, optical interconnect components (devices/modules, AOC, pre-terminated jumpers, high-speed copper cables). 2025 revenue RMB 14.25B, 30.7% overall GM. Overseas revenue RMB 6.09B (+47.8%, 42.7% of total).

**Three investable legs:**

**Leg A — Global fiber/preform capacity.** Integrated PCVD + VAD/OVD preform production. Real barrier at preform layer, but multiple large players exist (Chinese, Japanese incumbents). Cyclical/capacity-sensitive window, not permanent chokepoint.

**Leg B — Hollow-core fiber (HCF).** The most interesting part. HollowBand HCF achievements: cumulative delivery >10,000 core-km, attenuation 0.04 dB/km, max draw length 91.2 km, longest commercial route 100 km, 13+ commercial/pilot deployments globally, China Telecom live-network WDM trial 206.5 km / 51.3 Tbps (EDFA only, no remote pump). Few meaningful suppliers globally (Lightera/OFS, YOFC, Hengtong, Microsoft-acquired Lumenisity). HCF matters for AI datacenter interconnect, DCI, ultra-low-latency routes — the upside option, not yet a bankable revenue line.

**Leg C — AI optical interconnect components.** Changxinbochuang / 长芯博创 (tied to 300548.SZ): Google supply-chain beneficiary per local sources (MPO connectors, AOC connectors/modules, Marvell 1.6T AEC in testing). 2025 revenue RMB 2.53B, NP RMB 490M, segment grew +48.6%. **THE TRAP:** YOFC controls 300548 but holds only **19.0% economic interest** — minority shareholders hold 81.0% (minority income RMB 209.6M, minority equity RMB 2.17B). Cannot give 6869.HK shareholders full credit for consolidated optical-interconnect revenue. Ceiling uses economic-ownership-adjusted figures.
