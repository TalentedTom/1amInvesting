## Company Description

Chongqing International Composite Materials (CPIC / 国际复材) is the world's #3 glass fiber producer. Three segments: wind energy glass fiber (25%+ global share, high/ultra-high modulus), electronic glass fiber (low dielectric for 5G/AI PCBs, ultra-fine 3.7μm yarns), and thermosetting composites (insulation, optical cable reinforcement). Founded 1991, listed ChiNext December 2023.

## AI Connection — Electronic Glass Fiber

CPIC produces electronic-grade glass fiber yarn used in PCB manufacturing. AI servers require 30+ layer high-speed PCBs with very low dielectric loss tangent. These PCBs need advanced E-glass fiber cloth. CPIC makes the underlying glass fiber yarn. F16 electronic-grade line completed 2025. Low dielectric fiber used in Huawei 5G products. "Revitalize electronics" is a 2026 strategic priority.

## Why It FAILS

1. FOUR LAYERS REMOVED: Glass fiber yarn → cloth → PCB → server → AI. Framework invests at Layer 1 (lasers, substrates, test equipment), not Layer 4.

2. COMMODITY, NO MONOPOLY: China Jushi (#1), OCV/Owens Corning (#2), CPIC (#3), Taishan (#4). Zero pricing power. No sole-source.

3. CYCLICAL RECOVERY: FY2024 loss → FY2025 profit = glass fiber cycle recovering from oversupply trough, NOT structural AI-driven growth.

4. ELECTRONIC E-GLASS IS MINORITY: Wind energy and thermosetting composites dominate revenue. Can't value company on AI PCB thesis alone.
