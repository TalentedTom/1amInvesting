## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (Compound Semiconductor Epiwafer Foundry)

## Core Thesis: The Western Epi Layer, Named and Funded

IQE is the world's largest independent compound-semiconductor epiwafer foundry — it grows the atomically precise InP, GaAs, and GaN crystal layers that become lasers, photodetectors, RF amplifiers, and infrared sensors. The layer itself is now named: Tier-0 analysis puts the substrate/epi world (AXT, Sumitomo, IQE) at the **#1 bottleneck category** in the AI supply chain, and IQE is "one of the most important players in the Western world for epiwafers" — directly upstream of LITE, the largest optical company on earth.

The class-changing event: **MACOM invested £45M into IQE to secure InP epiwafer capacity** for CPO and pluggable lasers — customer-funded survival, the same pattern as hyperscalers funding memory fabs, now operating at the epi layer. A major customer has paid to ensure IQE survives and scales; the financial-distress discount is being retired by the demand side. Institutional discovery has begun (Point 72 accumulating).

**And the company is for sale.** Lazard is advising a Strategic Review with non-binding offers for the group as a whole plus separate bids for the Taiwan operations. The position classification follows: event-driven — the M&A is the single trigger inside a 6-12 month window, while the InP thesis and the MACOM floor cap the downside.

## IQE in the Supply Chain

```
AXTI grows InP/GaAs substrates (crystal boules)
  └── IQE grows the epitaxial layers ON those substrates
      └── chipmakers process epiwafers into lasers (COHR, LITE, AAOI chains),
          photodetectors, RF amps, IR sensors
```

The epi layer is where the device physics is actually created — and IQE is the largest independent house doing it in the West.
