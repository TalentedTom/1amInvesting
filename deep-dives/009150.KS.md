## Supercycle: DB5 AI Chip Packaging (passives/substrate) + DB1 CPO (substrate)

## Core Thesis: Power-Integrity and Package-Substrate Gatekeeper for AI Systems

Transitioned from "MLCC + camera module + substrate supplier" into a multi-layer AI-package platform: AI-server MLCC (~40% share), FC-BGA (three-player supply structure with Ibiden and Unimicron), silicon capacitors (>100x lower ESL/ESR than MLCC — inside-package power noise suppression for GPUs/HBM/AI ASICs), and glass-substrate option. 2025 record revenue ₩11.3145T (+y/y), driven by high-end AI/server/auto. Q1 2026: revenue ₩3.2091T, OP ₩280.6B (despite ₩71.4B one-time retirement expense).

**Key contracts:**
- **₩453.995B AI-server MLCC supply contract** with a global big-tech customer for CY2027 (~4.0% of 2025 revenue). Reportedly includes high-capacitance 47µF products and turnkey AI-server supply. Rule 16b-positive: big-tech locking passive components, not just GPUs and HBM.
- **~₩1.5T silicon-capacitor contract** with a global large company, Jan 1 2027 – Dec 31 2028. First large-scale SiCap achievement. Parts used inside GPU/HBM AI packages for power-supply stabilization. The Elec: market historically led by Murata/TSMC; Samsung won via precision process from MLCC/FC-BGA. Already supplied SiCap to Marvell for AI accelerator applications.

## Kill Switches

1. MLCC price hike fails — Murata or Taiwanese/Chinese capacity caps price, Component margin breaks
2. SiCap customer concentration — single 2-year contract, lumpy if platform shifts
3. FC-BGA capacity overshoots — Ibiden, Unimicron, AT&S all chasing the pool
4. Optical segment margin drag — large, lower-quality from bottleneck perspective
5. Glass slips or loses to organic panel packaging
6. Customer direct-sourcing or in-house packaging shifts value away
