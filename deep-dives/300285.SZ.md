## Supercycle: MLCC Materials Chokepoint

## Core Thesis: China's Murata of Ceramic Powder — 80-90% Domestic Share, >30% Global

Sinocera is the dominant global supplier of MLCC dielectric ceramic powder (barium titanate). Global share >30% (vs Sakai Chemical ~25%). Chinese domestic share 80-90% — near-monopoly. Every Chinese MLCC maker expanding capacity buys powder from Sinocera. Samsung Electro-Mechanics (SEMCO) is the #1 customer (>30% of revenue), with Yageo and Fenghua also confirmed clients.

Source: 国投证券 research report (May 18, 2026), company investor relations (April 20, 2026 analyst meeting), 财联社 (May 15, 2026).

The thesis: as Tier 1 MLCC makers (Murata, Taiyo Yuden, SEMCO) move upmarket to AI server MLCCs and expand capacity, demand for Sinocera's powder scales with every factory expansion. Murata already raised AI server MLCC prices +25% in Q2 2026 — that pricing power flows upstream to the powder supplier. Every MLCC capacity expansion globally requires more barium titanate powder, and Sinocera + Sakai Chemical control >55% of the world's supply.

## New Growth Vectors (beyond MLCC)

### Satellite ceramic tube shells (国瓷赛创):
LEO satellite ceramic packaging has become the "mainstream packaging solution" — 2025 revenue grew rapidly, 2026 continuing to seize market opportunities. European IRIS² infrastructure + China space program = dual demand driver. Phase 2 factory construction complete, capacity ramping.

### Solid-state battery electrolyte:
Automated sulfide solid-state electrolyte production line completed. Products sent to downstream customers for validation. "Customer performance recognition high." JV with Dr. Wang Yan for sulfide route R&D. Also developing oxide-route (zirconate-based) electrolyte. Pre-revenue but positioned for the next battery transition.

### Optical module ceramic substrates (TEC):
Small batch sales to initial customers, new customers still in validation. Connected to the photonics supply chain — TEC substrates used in optical transceivers. Phase 2 clean room completed.

## Key Risks

1. **Sakai Chemical (4078.T)** holds ~25% global share — Sinocera is not a monopoly. Competition is real at the high end.
2. **Commodity-adjacent** — ceramic powder is an input, not a differentiated product. Switching costs exist (qualification cycles) but are lower than semiconductor-grade chokepoints.
3. **Samsung concentration** — SEMCO is >30% of revenue. Single-customer dependency.
4. **Raw material costs** — barium carbonate, titanium dioxide, nickel prices affect margins.
5. **Fire incident (April 7, 2026)** — one factory damaged, not yet in production use. Limited impact but flagged.
6. **Chinese market listing** — 100% maintenance margin on IBKR, limited Western investor access.
