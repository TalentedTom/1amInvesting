## Supercycle: MLCC Materials (BT Powder — Layer -1 of the passive chain)

## Core Thesis: China's Powder Near-Monopoly, the World's #1 Share

Sinocera is the dominant global supplier of MLCC dielectric ceramic powder (barium titanate): **>30% global share** (vs Sakai's ~25% — together >55% of world supply) and **80-90% Chinese domestic share** — a near-monopoly at home. Samsung Electro-Mechanics is the #1 customer (>30% of revenue, "deep binding... technology co-development partner" per 国投证券); Yageo and Fenghua confirmed clients. Every MLCC capacity expansion anywhere requires more powder, and the June 2026 channel confirms the wave is upstream-bound: **high-end MLCC structurally tight 1-2 years, 20+ week lead times, CSPs signing LTAs** — the squeeze reaches the powder layer last and holds it longest. Murata's +25% AI-server MLCC pricing (Q2 2026) is the flow-through to watch.
