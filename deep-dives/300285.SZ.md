## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

Sinocera supplies engineered powders and ceramics whose consistency, purity and qualification matter to customers. MLCC dielectrics are one engine within a diversified group; dental, catalysts, battery coatings and printing materials are already substantial. The completed SDI acquisition adds clinical dental products and distribution. Spherical silica, solid-state electrolyte and AI-server ceramics can enlarge the opportunity, but development and small-batch products cannot be treated as mature volume revenue.

Raw oxides and chemical inputs → controlled powder synthesis and ceramic processing → capacitor, battery, dental, catalyst and industrial customers. Qualification and consistency matter. AI server demand reaches powder suppliers through actual qualified material consumption, not the full selling price or component count of every capacitor. SDI adds clinical consumables downstream of the legacy dental-laboratory materials business.
