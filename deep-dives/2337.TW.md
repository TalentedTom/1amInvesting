## Supercycle: Memory Structural — The Abandoned Commodity Winner

## Core Thesis: Last Producer Standing

The textbook abandoned-commodity pattern: the Big Four redirect wafer capacity to HBM and 3D NAND (70-80% margins), the legacy segment's supply collapses, and the last scale producer inherits monopoly pricing. Chairman Miin Wu: "Macronix is the only company globally capable of supplying this specification range at scale." The company moved from quarterly to **monthly pricing** — the hallmark of monopoly power — with NOR +30% in Q1 and SLC NAND/NOR pricing expected +100% by Q2.

**The supply cliff (triple-sourced, May 2026):** Samsung shutting its LAST 2D NAND fab (Hwaseong Line 12, 80-100K wpm → 1c DRAM conversion; final MLC shipments June 2026, permanent). Kioxia phasing out ALL 2D NAND + 3rd-gen BiCS — **final orders September 2026**, shipments end 2028, full exit 2029. Micron at minimum levels, Crucial winding down. TrendForce: MLC capacity -41.7% in 2026. MLC 64Gb spot $20-28, **+300% from end-2024** — the DDR4 pattern ($1.35→$16) repeating. Disappearance test: PASS — the eMMC supply chain collapses without Macronix.

**The honest classification is temporal:** the moat is economic, not physical. Nothing in physics stops GigaDevice or new Chinese legacy fabs from rebuilding this capacity — it simply isn't rational while AI absorbs every new wafer. The window is real, the expiration date is visible (~2-3 years), and the score carries both.
