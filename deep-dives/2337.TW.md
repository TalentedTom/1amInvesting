## Supercycle: Memory Structural — Abandoned Commodity Winner 🔒

## Classification: CHOKEPOINT

Macronix is the textbook "abandoned commodity winner" — the framework pattern where Big Four memory makers redirect capacity to AI-premium products (HBM, 3D NAND), creating a supply vacuum in legacy segments that the last scale producer fills with monopoly pricing.

---

## The Thesis

### What Happened
Samsung announced June 2026 as its final MLC eMMC shipment date. SK Hynix, Micron, and Kioxia are also limiting or exiting MLC NAND production. TrendForce projects MLC NAND capacity will DECREASE 41.7% in 2026. The Big Four are permanently redirecting wafer capacity to HBM and 3D NAND where margins are 70-80% — they will NEVER come back to MLC.

### Why Macronix Wins
Chairman Miin Wu: "Macronix is the only company globally capable of supplying this specification range at scale."

Macronix has become the sole-source supplier of low-density MLC NAND (used in eMMC) outside the Big Four. With all four exiting, Macronix inherits a $1-2B market with near-zero competition. The company has moved from quarterly to MONTHLY pricing — the hallmark of monopoly power. NOR Flash prices hiked 30% in Q1; SLC NAND and NOR prices expected to jump 100% by Q2 2026.

### The Numbers

Q1 2026: Revenue TWD 10.5B (+71% YoY), GM 40.8% (from 17% YoY), OP margin 18.5%, Net Profit TWD 1.78B (EPS TWD 0.90). Ended ten-quarter losing streak. NAND revenue +382% YoY, +90% QoQ. eMMC revenue +94% QoQ, ~40x YoY.

April 2026: Revenue TWD 5.91B (+153.7% YoY, +33.7% MoM) = ALL-TIME HIGH. YTD Jan-Apr: TWD 16.38B (+93.5% YoY). Q2 estimate: TWD 17.15B.

Chairman: "Q1 gross margin is just the starting point. There will be greater room for growth going forward."

### Revenue Mix Shift
NOR Flash: 58.5% of Q1 revenue (+47% YoY). NAND Flash: 30% of Q1 (+382% YoY — explosive growth driver). ROM: 8% (declining). Target: NOR:NAND ratio moving from 1:0.5 toward 1:1.

### Pricing Power
Monthly pricing (not quarterly) = monopoly behavior. NOR +30% Q1, continuing. SLC NAND + NOR expected +100% by Q2. Chairman: "Price increases cannot be unlimited" — but DIRECTION is clear.

## Supply Cliff — Three of Four Suppliers Exiting (May 2026)

The MLC/2D NAND supply cliff is deeper than initially modeled. Not just MLC exit — the ENTIRE 2D NAND category is collapsing:

**Samsung:** Shutting LAST 2D NAND fab (Hwaseong Line 12, 80-100K wafers/month). Converting to 1c DRAM. MLC final shipments June 2026. PERMANENT EXIT. Source: Jukan (May 12, 2026).

**Kioxia:** Phasing out ALL 2D NAND + 3rd-gen BiCS Flash. Final orders September 2026. Final shipments December 2028. Full exit 2029. Source: Jukan (May 12, 2026).

**Micron:** Maintaining MLC at MINIMUM levels only. Winding down Crucial brand. Not investing in legacy capacity. Source: Jukan (May 12, 2026).

**Pricing confirmation:** MLC 64Gb spot: $20-28 range (+300% from end-2024). Same abandoned commodity pattern as DDR4 ($1.35→$16). Monthly pricing = monopoly behavior. April 2026 revenue TWD 5.91B (+153.7% YoY) = ALL-TIME HIGH.

THREE of four major suppliers now exiting. Macronix "only company globally" supplying MLC NAND/eMMC at scale. Thesis PROVEN in real-time.
