## Thesis

Three growth engines driving simultaneous revenue explosion:

**Engine 1: DDR4 Abandoned Commodity (Rule 5)**
Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron all issued Last-Time-Buy on DDR4. Industrial customers locked into DDR4 for 5-10 year qualification cycles. Innodisk secured EXCLUSIVE supply from Nanya Tech (2409) and Winbond (2344). Competitors cannot access the same DDR4 chips. Monthly pricing with sustained hikes. MLC NAND capacity decreasing 41.7% in 2026 (TrendForce).

**Engine 2: CXL Memory Expansion**
CXL E3.S memory modules sampling Q1 2026, revenue H2 2026-2027. Memory pooling/disaggregation for AI servers. Pricing based on "solution value" not "chip cost + margin" = structurally higher margins. One of very few industrial CXL module makers globally.

**Engine 3: Edge AI Platform (Aetina)**
Subsidiary Aetina (安提國際) builds edge AI systems. NVIDIA Jetson Thor distributor. Penetrated humanoid robot and industrial automation supply chains. Taiwanese analysts: "Compare to Advantech (研華), not ADATA." Platform company, not just module assembler.

## Moat Assessment

- #1 global industrial SSD provider (Gartner, 8 consecutive years)
- EXCLUSIVE DDR4 supply from Nanya Tech + Winbond (competitors locked out)
- 18-36 month qualification cycles (defense, aerospace, medical)
- Consumer module GM: 10-15%. Innodisk historical GM: 28-32%. Gap = pricing power.
- BUT: module assembly, not chip fabrication. Not physics-based moat.
- Disappearance test: PARTIAL pass (18-36 month disruption, not permanent)
