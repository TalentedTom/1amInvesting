## Company Description

Suzhou Kechuan Electronic Technology is a functional films and materials company serving battery and consumer electronics industries. In February 2024, it established a subsidiary 可川光子 (Kechuan Photon) to enter silicon photonics chips and optical modules. The subsidiary has completed first SiPh chip tapeout (100G single-channel) and built a 400G/800G production line (40-50K modules/month capacity) that is in commissioning stage. Products are in "sending samples" phase with no confirmed orders.
