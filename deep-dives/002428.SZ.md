## 1. Supercycle, supply chain and durable advantage

AI optical links need qualified compound-semiconductor substrates, while space power and fiber systems consume germanium-based materials. Yunnan Germanium has established production and customer validation, but substrates, epitaxy, laser devices and modules are separate markets. Demand growth expands its opportunity without proving exclusivity, guaranteeing higher InP content per module, or turning customer certification into a purchase commitment.

Raw germanium/indium/phosphorus → purified material/crystal → qualified substrate → epitaxy → device → optical module or solar cell. Module growth is several steps downstream; customer yields, wafer size and die design affect substrate demand. Revenue does not equal all module-market value.
