## Supercycle: DB11 Materials Escalation Regime + DB1 Photonics

## Core Thesis: China's Only 6-Inch InP Substrate Mass Producer

Yunnan Germanium is a legacy germanium miner that owns 鑫耀半导体 (Xinyao Semiconductor), China's dominant InP substrate producer and the ONLY domestic company mass-producing 6-inch InP wafers. Huawei Hubble holds 23.91% of the subsidiary as second-largest shareholder — a strategic lock-in binding InP substrate supply to China's sovereign AI compute buildout.

**The InP position (from Chinese sources, 东方财富/新浪/证券时报):**

Domestic market share >80% for InP substrates. Current capacity 15万片/year (2-4 inch equivalent). Expansion to 45万片/year announced April 2026 (¥1.89亿 investment), with 6-inch comprising >60%. 2027 target: 100万片/year. 6-inch yield at 70-85% and improving (Sumitomo ~90%, the gap to close). Dislocation density <500/cm² (Sumitomo ~300/cm², domestic competitors >1,000/cm²).

**Customer lock-in:** Huawei has locked 15万片 6-inch on long-term contract, with 40% prepayment (industry standard <20%). Orders locked through 2027 Q2. Additional customers: 中际旭创 (InnoLight), 光迅科技 (Accelink), 源杰科技 (Yuanjie). All are domestic Chinese optical module/chip makers who CANNOT source InP from Sumitomo/AXT/JX due to export controls. 云南锗业 is their ONLY option at 6-inch scale.

**Why 6-inch matters:** 6-inch InP wafer pricing at $5,000/wafer (+250% since controls began per DB11). The 6-inch-to-4-inch die yield advantage is ~2.25x, reducing per-chip substrate cost by 60-70% — the same economics that drove the silicon wafer transition. Companies qualifying 6-inch NOW will lock out competitors for 12-18 months (qualification cycle). 云南锗业 is 12-18 months ahead of all domestic competitors on 6-inch.

**The global context (DB11):** Global InP supply: Sumitomo ~40%, AXT ~35%, JX ~10%, Others ~15%. Global capacity ~300万片 (4-inch equivalent). China domestic capacity ~30-33万片 = ~10% of global. But China's DEMAND is exploding (Source Photonics, InnoLight, Huawei all expanding). The gap is structural — new InP crystal growth capacity takes 2.5-3 years to bring online.

**The vulnerability:** Q1 2026 revenue ¥2.89亿 (+20.31%), net profit ¥910万 (-10.71%), gross margin 20.29%. Deducted net profit for FY2025 was NEGATIVE (¥-680万). This company is barely profitable despite sitting on a monopoly. Legacy germanium products (锗矿, 红外光学锗) are low-margin commodities dragging the blended results. The InP thesis requires MASSIVE execution on the 6-inch ramp to overcome the legacy drag.

## Cross-Book Read-Throughs

**DB11 validation:** 云南锗业 IS the Chinese side of the InP bifurcation thesis already in DB11. Their expansion from 15→100万片 is the domestic response to the export control regime. If they succeed, China's InP self-sufficiency improves, potentially REDUCING the scarcity premium that benefits IQE, AXT, and COHR's substrate operations.

**IQE:** Direct competitor in InP epiwafers (different product — IQE does epitaxy on substrates, 云南锗业 makes the substrates themselves). But both benefit from InP shortage. 云南锗业 expansion could increase substrate availability for Chinese epi houses (VPEC, domestic competitors to IQE), reducing pressure on IQE's Western supply. Marginally negative for IQE long-term if China achieves substrate self-sufficiency.

**SIVE/LITE/COHR:** These companies' laser chips are fabricated ON InP substrates. More Chinese substrate supply = more Chinese laser production capacity = potential for Chinese competitors to SIVE/LITE/COHR in the laser space. Long-term competitive threat, but qualification cycles are 3-5 years at the laser level.

**Dongshan/Source Photonics:** Confirmed customer of 云南锗业 for InP substrates. The supply chain link is: 云南锗业 (substrate) → Source Photonics (EML chip fabrication on the substrate) → optical modules. 云南锗业's expansion directly enables Source Photonics' EML ramp.

**AXT (screen queue):** AXT is the global #2 InP producer (~35% share), China-manufactured but US-headquartered, caught inside the export control gate. 云南锗业 is the domestic ALTERNATIVE to AXT for Chinese buyers. The two companies are substitutes inside China; AXT serves the rest of the world. November 10 policy expiry affects both.

## Key Risks (each scored)

1. **Execution risk (-1):** 6-inch yields at 70-75% need to reach 85%+ for the margin model to work. Current revenue is ¥2.89亿/quarter with near-zero profit. The gap between the thesis (domestic InP monopoly) and the financials (PE >2,000x on negative deducted profits) is the widest in the book. If 6-inch expansion delays or yields stall, the stock collapses.

2. **Valuation risk (-1):** FY2025 deducted net profit was NEGATIVE. Q1 2026 net profit was ¥910万 on ¥542亿 MC = >5,900x annualized PE. The stock gained 47% in 6 days (证券时报). A-share retail speculation has fully absorbed the thesis. Any execution disappointment meets maximum downside.

3. **Policy/competition risk (-1):** If November 10 InP export controls expire without renewal, Sumitomo/AXT/JX regain access to Chinese customers, destroying the domestic premium. Simultaneously, 鼎泰芯源 (Dingtai, under 博杰股份 002975) is expanding toward 20万片/year with 6-inch in R&D. 先导微电子 has announced 6-inch capability. The domestic monopoly is durable only as long as the combination of export controls + 12-18 month qualification cycles holds.

## Re-Rate Triggers

1. **6-inch yield milestone (85%+ sustained):** Proves mass production viability. GM jumps from 35% to 50%+. Financial inflection point. COUNTER: yields plateau at 75-80%.

2. **November 10, 2026 InP export control renewal:** Binary. Renewal = another leg of scarcity, domestic premium extends. Expiry/relaxation = domestic premium compresses, stock at risk. This is the single most important catalyst for the entire InP chain.

3. **Quarterly revenue inflection (InP >50% of total):** When InP revenue exceeds legacy germanium, the blended margins transform and the company re-rates from "germanium miner" to "semiconductor materials." Q1 2026 InP revenue was not separately disclosed — the first segmented print is the tell.

4. **Huawei order expansion beyond 15万片:** If Huawei increases the 6-inch order (from 15万→25万+), validates both demand and yield quality. COUNTER: Huawei develops second-source.

5. **MONOPOLY FALSIFICATION:** 鼎泰芯源 achieves 6-inch mass production → domestic monopoly breaks. Currently 6-inch is "R&D verification" stage at competitors; this trigger has a 12-18 month minimum timeline.
