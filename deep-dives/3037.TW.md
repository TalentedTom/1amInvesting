## Supercycle: DB5 AI Packaging Materials (ABF Substrates — The Packaging Bottleneck)

## Core Thesis: Every AI Chip Needs ABF Substrates — And They're Sold Out

Every NVIDIA GPU, every AMD AI accelerator, every Google TPU, every custom ASIC needs ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates for its FC-BGA package. Unimicron is the world's #1 ABF substrate maker in a concentrated oligopoly where the top 5 hold ~60% of capacity. ABF substrates are SOLD OUT. ASPs rose 26% in a year ($65→$82). Lead times stretch to 28 weeks. And CoWoS packaging — which sits on top of ABF substrates — is now tighter than leading-edge wafer fab.

## The VOLUME × ASP × CONTENT Triple-Whammy

This is the Goldman Sachs ABF thesis from our framework:

1. **VOLUME:** AI accelerator shipments doubling every 2 years (1.2M units in 2025). Each unit needs 4-6 FC-BGA substrates.
2. **ASP:** Prices climbing 26% YoY ($65→$82) as supply lags demand. Goldman data shows +30-40% QoQ increases.
3. **CONTENT:** AI chips getting larger → more substrate area per chip. Layer counts increasing (10-14 layers standard, 14+ for cutting-edge AI). Each generation needs MORE substrate.

When all three vectors compound simultaneously, revenue growth accelerates non-linearly.

## The Glass Substrate Optionality (Corning JV)

Unimicron's $180M investment in Corning's glass-substrate pilot (30% stake) is a forward-looking bet. Glass cores offer:
- Ultra-low warpage for sub-4nm devices
- Optical routing capability for co-packaged optics
- Better thermal performance at extreme power densities (700W+)
- 7.47% CAGR growth projected

If glass substrates replace organic ABF for next-gen AI chips, Unimicron has secured its position in BOTH technologies. This is the Rule 11 (Acquire the Bottleneck) move — buy into the technology that could eventually displace your current product.
