### Google/SpaceX Pricing Power Revision (Jun 19, 2026)

**THE EVIDENCE:** On Jun 5, 2026, Google signed a Cloud Service Agreement with SpaceX: $920M/month for ~110,000 NVIDIA GPUs from October 2026 through June 2029 (~33 months). Google called it "short-term bridge capacity" for Gemini Enterprise demand "higher than expected."

**THE MATH:** $920M/month ÷ 110K GPUs ÷ 720 hrs = **$11.61/GPU/hr.** Anthropic pays SpaceX $1.25B/month for 220K GPUs = **$7.89/GPU/hr.** Current neocloud blended pricing: **~$4-5/hr** (SemiAnalysis: H100 1yr contracts at $2.35/hr, B200 mean $5.09/hr).

**THE IMPLICATION:** Google — the most sophisticated compute buyer on earth, operating some of the largest DCs globally — is willing to pay **2-3x current neocloud rates** for guaranteed capacity. And they signed for **3 years.** This is not a spot premium. This is structural demand validation through 2029.

**THE CONSERVATIVE REPRICING:** If NBIS moves from ~$5/hr blended to $6.50/hr (30% increase), that is STILL 44% BELOW Google's rate. We are not modeling Google-level pricing. We are modeling the FLOOR that Google's deal establishes — if the market leader pays $11.61, the floor for contracted capacity is $6-7, not $4-5. SemiAnalysis confirms: "we just raised prices again, still selling out at higher prices."

**WHY MARGINS EXPAND:** Higher GPU-hour pricing on FIXED infrastructure costs (power, cooling, rent, depreciation) = pure operating leverage. Every incremental dollar of pricing flows almost entirely to OP. The 30% revenue uplift produces ~50-68% OP uplift because the cost base doesn't scale with pricing.
