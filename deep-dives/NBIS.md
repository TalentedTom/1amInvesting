NBIS holds the only credible EU-domiciled hyperscale AI cloud (Amsterdam HQ, Finnish DCs) in a regime where the EU AI Act and GDPR bar European governments from US hyperscalers for sensitive workloads.

**The Q1 2026 reclassification — customer → compute delivery bottleneck:** "4+ customers competing for every GPU we bring online."
