## Why The Framework Scores BE Low Despite Incredible Fundamentals

The fundamentals are genuinely strong. $20B backlog. Oracle 2.8 GW. AEP $2.65B. Revenue doubling. Technology proven. But:

1. **$50B market cap:** The framework prefers small-caps where information asymmetry exists. At $50B with 20+ analysts, there is ZERO information edge. Every institutional investor on Earth knows the Bloom thesis.

2. **1,100% already ran:** The re-rating from "$16 niche fuel cell company" to "$217 AI infrastructure giant" already happened. The easy money is made. What's left is execution premium on a 119x P/E.

3. **Entry ratio compressed:** $217 vs ceiling midpoint $400 = 1.84x. The framework needs 2.5x+ for WL. Even the bull case ($500) gives only 2.3x. Compare to SIVE at 6.6x or AEHR at 4.3x.

4. **Not a physics monopoly:** Gas turbines from GE/Siemens, nuclear from NuScale/Oklo, and battery+solar from Tesla/Fluence all compete for the same datacenter power dollar. Bloom has a TIME advantage (90 days), not a physics barrier.
