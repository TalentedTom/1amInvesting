## HPS in the AI Infrastructure Stack

Every megawatt of AI compute needs electrical distribution:

```
120-150 GW datacenter compute by 2030
  └── needs power distribution at each facility
      └── needs transformers (HPS) to step down voltage
      └── needs switchgear and substations
      └── needs HVDC cables (NKT) for long-distance
      └── needs VPD (VICR) at chip level
```

HPS is the facility-level power distribution layer. VICR is the package-level. NKT is the transmission-level. All three are needed; they don't compete.
