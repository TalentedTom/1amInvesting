## Why 61 and Not Higher

The base at 63% is the honest limiter. PL is Path B competitive — Maxar, BlackSky, Capella, Umbra all compete in earth observation. The framework asks "if PL disappeared, could defense customers get satellite imagery?" and the answer is yes, from multiple providers. The defense contracts are large and growing, but they're won through competition, not through physics-required monopoly positioning.

The TAM math gives 2.68x to ceiling midpoint — moderate asymmetry. If PL develops proprietary AI analytics that become non-substitutable, or if satellite services contracts create genuine lock-in, the base score improves.
