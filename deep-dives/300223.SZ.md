## Supercycle: Memory Upcycle (DB6) + Automotive Intelligence + China Localization

## Core Thesis: Global Car-Grade SRAM #1 + DRAM #4 Riding the Slowest, Longest Storage Pricing Cycle

北京君正 is a fabless IC designer with three product lines: storage chips (65% rev), compute chips (27%), and analog & interconnect chips (11%). The critical distinction from GigaDevice: **automotive focus.** Car-grade SRAM global #1 (20-35% share), car-grade DRAM global #4 (behind Micron, Samsung, Nanya), car-grade LPDDR4/4X sole domestic supplier. Platform: "存储+计算+模拟."

**Q1 2026 inflection (一季报):** Revenue ¥15.60亿 (+47% YoY, +20% QoQ). NI ¥3.19亿 (+332% YoY). Single quarter exceeded FY2025 full-year non-GAAP NI (¥3.09亿). Blended GM 43.49% (from 34% FY2025). Three-expense ratio compressed 27.73%→19.64% (operating leverage).

By segment:
- Storage: ¥10.18亿 (+53.6% YoY), **GM 38.89%**
- Compute: ¥4.03亿 (+49.1% YoY), **GM 51.90%**
- Analog: ¥1.32亿 (+11.0% YoY), **GM 50.88%**

**Why automotive storage has the LONGEST cycle:** Car-grade pricing transmits slowly (long-term agreements, qualification barriers). Management (Mar 2026): "对于行业的DRAM来说，2026年才是周期真正的开始." Q1 had domestic repricing only; Q2-Q3 international clients execute new prices. Each quarter brings a new price round. 智通财经: "业绩释放是持续增长型，而非脉冲式爆发."

**July 2026 线控转向强标:** Mandatory wire-controlled steering requires ECU dual SRAM redundancy. Per-car SRAM value DOUBLES. As global car-grade SRAM #1, direct regulatory demand catalyst.

**FY2025 full year:** Revenue ¥47.41亿 (+12.5%). NI ¥3.76亿 (+2.7%). Operating cash flow ¥6.23亿 (+71%). Storage ¥29.11亿 (61.4%). Compute ¥12.93亿 (27.3%). Analog ¥5.06亿 (10.7%).

## Key Risks

1. **Supply-side reflexivity (-1):** The shortage is built on big-3 "弃低追高." If Samsung/Micron/SKH re-enter niche DRAM (which they WILL eventually), the pricing premium collapses. 智通财经: "而这一天，终将到来."

2. **Insider selling (-1):** Multiple rounds — controller 李杰, PE funds (屹唐盛芯 1%, 武岳峰 1%, 四海君芯 0.65%), high management. Cumulative significant. 1.40% additional pre-disclosed Apr 7, 2026.

3. **Fabless dependency (-1):** No owned fab. Foundry diversification not disclosed. If capacity allocation is reduced, revenue is gated.

4. **A-share valuation (-1):** ~85x annualized Q1 NI. ¥1,086亿 MC on ¥3.19亿 quarterly NI. Under 20x OP, deeply negative EV.
