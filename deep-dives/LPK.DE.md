## Core Thesis: The Leading Formation Tool in a Multi-Vendor Glass Transition

LIDE (Laser-Induced Deep Etching): laser modifies precise paths inside glass, chemistry etches them — crack-free, high-precision TGVs, cavities, and microstructures with a **10x sidewall-quality advantage** over competing methods and up to 1:50 aspect ratio. Patent protected (European + Korean) plus years of process recipes, throughput optimization, and defect control.

CEO Klaus Fiedler (The Elec, April 20, 2026): **"More than 80% of major global players have selected our equipment for process validation, learning and scaling to mass production"** — with initial production orders already recorded in Q1 2026 and South Korea the leading country.

**v2 reframe (Jul 2, 2026):** qualification breadth (real, 80%) does NOT convert to production-share monopoly. The industry's commercial-readiness benchmark sits near 6µm vias / 15:1 AR (Future Markets 2026) — well inside LIDE's headline 1:50 physics. At required specs the formation step is **multi-vendor in both key regions**: Taiwan (Innolux+ITRI+Contrel AR-25 process, published; 鈦昇/8027 already delivering into the TSMC CoPoS chain) and Korea (F&S Electron incumbent at Absolics until Mar 2026 exclusivity expiry, now dual-source; JNTC vertically integrated with self-built tools at ~1/5 industry capex). The prior "LIDE : glass :: EUV : silicon" framing is **retired** — EUV had no alternative at required specs; LIDE has several. LPKF's edge is maturity, throughput, sidewall quality, installed qualification base, and the paired SCHMID/Atotech workflow — a leading-vendor position, not a gate.

The financials remain the discount: FY2025 €115.3M (-6.2%), adjusted EBIT €0.8M; FY2026 guide €105-120M **explicitly excluding** potential high-volume advanced packaging orders; North Star restructuring targets double-digit EBIT by 2028. The framework buys a leading share of an exploding equipment TAM at industrial-company pricing.

## Jul 6, 2026 — CoPoS Glass-Category Timing Reset (v2.2)

**What changed:** local Taiwanese reporting and supply-chain corrections separate three different “glass” positions that the market had conflated: **glass carriers** (temporary manufacturing support, low value), **glass-core substrates / GCS** (final package substrate core, high value, TGV-critical), and **glass interposers** (not a TSMC CoPoS roadmap item). Economic Daily/TrendForce frame TSMC’s near-term CoPoS around 310×310mm validation in 2026, pilot in 2027, formal MP in 2H28, while the **next phase shifts to glass-core substrates with mass production expected after 2030**. The same report explicitly says CoPoS uses **glass carriers** and that future GCS/TGV maturity governs yield. A July 4 鉅亨/Tech Taiwan supply-chain correction says first-generation CoPoS may be effectively **“glass-free” in the final package**, with glass used as a temporary carrier; it also says TSMC has not put **glass interposers** on the CoPoS roadmap.

**Source weighting:** UDN/Economic Daily + TrendForce timing = high for roadmap direction; 鉅亨/Tech Taiwan supply-chain correction = medium-high as category taxonomy / channel correction. Combined read: **near-term TSMC CoPoS remains real, but near-term high-value GCS/LIDE production for the TSMC chain is pushed right.**

**Model impact:** the long-term LIDE/GCS thesis stays alive, but the FY2028-2029 TSMC-chain GCS contribution is no longer modeled as production. Glass-carrier exposure is not credited because it is temporary tooling, not the high-value glass-core substrate. V8 “glass interposer” is retired from revenue until a non-TSMC roadmap or product explicitly revives it. This is a timing/category reset, not a thesis break.

**Scoring impact:** Cycle 9→8 and Risks −3→−4. Base **47/60 (78%) → 45/60 (75%)**.

## Multi-Vendor Reality (replaces "The ASML Parallel," retired Jul 2 2026)

At commercial-readiness specs (≈6µm / 15:1 AR), formation is contested: LIDE (spec leader), Contrel/ITRI (AR 25, Taiwan chain), 鈦昇 (delivering into CoPoS), F&S Electron (Absolics incumbent), JNTC (vertical bypass), photosensitive-glass routes. LPKF's durable advantages: sidewall quality at density (matters most for GCS/waveguides — V2/V9), throughput at scale, paired SCHMID/Atotech metallization workflow, installed qualification base, patent estate (enforcement scope vs Taiwan/Korea implementations unproven). The industry gate remains **metallization yield at panel scale** (Cu fill, CpK 1.33 — DB5), which no formation vendor solves alone: Taiwanese press confirms yield (micro-cracks, Cu-fill duration, CD uniformity, transparent-media AOI) is why MP waits.

## Sovereignty Thesis

European-headquartered leader with the broadest global glass-packaging qualification base. A hostile acquisition (FiconTEC pattern) would cost the West its glass-equipment leader; retail ownership of European chokepoints creates the visibility that protects them.
