## Supercycle: DB5 AI Chip Packaging + DB1 CPO Photonics + DB3 LEO Space + DB8 Defense

## Core Thesis: The Universal Primitive for Glass Packaging

LIDE (Laser-Induced Deep Etching): laser modifies precise paths inside glass, chemistry etches them — crack-free, high-precision TGVs, cavities, and microstructures with a **10x sidewall-quality advantage** over competing methods. Patent protected (European + Korean) plus years of process recipes, throughput optimization, and defect control.

CEO Klaus Fiedler (The Elec, April 20, 2026): **"More than 80% of major global players have selected our equipment for process validation, learning and scaling to mass production"** — with initial production orders already recorded in Q1 2026 and South Korea the leading country. Qualification near-monopoly converts to an expected 40-60% production share.

Ford Principle: not "laser machines" — the enabling tool that makes the glass transition possible. **LIDE is to glass what EUV is to silicon.** Three supercycles from one primitive: AI packaging (Intel/Samsung/TSMC), space (SpaceX Starlink antennas via Schmid 700mm), defense.

The financials are the discount: FY2025 €115.3M (-6.2%), adjusted EBIT €0.8M; FY2026 guide €105-120M **explicitly excluding** potential high-volume advanced packaging orders; North Star restructuring targets double-digit EBIT by 2028. The framework buys the option at industrial-company pricing.

## Ecosystem Map (each entity once)

Fraunhofer IZM Glass Panel Technology Group founding member (1 of 15) | Schmid InfinityLine = paired wet-process workflow (LIDE TGV → etch/plating) | Intel: LIDE installed, glass-core substrates public | Samsung SEMCO: process designed around LPKF (The Elec) | TSMC: CoPoS 2H28 | Absolics/SKC, DNP, LG Innotek: 2027-28 MP | Unimicron-Corning glass JV ($180M, 30% stake) | Onto Innovation metrology integrated at Vitrion foundry.

**EIC→CPO→EPIC progression:** glass's relative advantage INCREASES each architectural generation — CTE/flatness vs organic at EIC; fiber-matched IOX waveguide (~9µm mode field) vs silicon-interposer mode mismatch at CPO; the only platform integrating TGV (electrical) + waveguide (optical) + PIC edge coupling in one substrate at EPIC.

**SOI.PA complement (PhotonCap):** glass packaging unlocks CPO yields → PIC demand rises → the starting wafer is photonics-SOI. Vertical complements; framework owns both.

Glass-core market: $244M (2024) → $460-586M (2030, Yole/QY Research); 80% share × ~25% equipment intensity ≈ $92-117M LIDE revenue from the substrate line alone.
