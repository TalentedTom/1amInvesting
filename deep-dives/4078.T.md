## Supercycle: MLCC Materials — Rule 10 (Path A inside Path B)

## Core Thesis: ~25% Global MLCC Dielectric Powder, Buried in a Conglomerate

Sakai Chemical is the world's second-largest supplier of MLCC dielectric powder (barium titanate), ~25% global share behind Sinocera (~30%), vertically integrated from raw-material sourcing (subsidiary Sakai Shoji) through high-purity powder. The powder thesis is real and current: every capacity expansion at Murata, Taiyo Yuden, or SEMCO requires this material; AI-server MLCC demand runs 80%+ CAGR; Murata raised AI-server MLCC prices +25% in Q2 2026; and the June 2026 supply picture confirms it upstream — **high-end MLCC structurally tight for 1-2 years, 20+ week lead times, CSPs signing LTAs** (the squeeze reaches the powder layer last and holds it longest).

**Rule 10 applies and caps the score:** Electronic Materials is one of eleven segments (~¥15-20B of ¥84B revenue, ~18-24%); the rest is titanium dioxide, cosmetics raw materials, Kaigen cold medicine, resin additives, catalysts, medical. The chokepoint is real; the wrapper trades on the other 76-82%. If the segment were spun off, it would score 80+; as a conglomerate, it is capped — and the cap is the trigger map.
