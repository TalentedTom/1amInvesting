## Company Description

Tiantong Holdings is a Chinese electronic materials and equipment company. Core businesses: magnetic materials (#1 soft ferrite in China), sapphire crystal, piezoelectric crystal, crystal growth equipment. The CPO connection is through lithium niobate (LiNbO₃) crystal materials for thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) optical modulators — a next-gen technology for ultra-high-speed datacom.
