## 800V DC + VPD — Complementary, Not Competitive

NVIDIA visiting Korean companies about 800V DC architecture is GOOD for VICR:

```
Traditional: AC → PDU → 48V → multiphase → 1V (to chip)
800V DC:     800V → 48V → multiphase → 1V (to chip)  
800V + VPD:  800V → VPD → 1V (directly underneath chip)
```

800V is facility-level distribution. VPD is package-level delivery. They're complementary layers. 800V makes the building more efficient. VPD makes the chip more efficient. Both are needed.
