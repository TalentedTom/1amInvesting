## Core Thesis: The Physics of Feeding a Chip

Ford Principle: not "power modules" — the physics of delivering power **vertically through the substrate, underneath the processor.** As chips grow larger, hotter, denser, lateral delivery physically fails (it cannot reach the center of a wafer-scale die); CPO seals the verdict by claiming the package top for optics, forcing power to the bottom. The rack trajectory makes it structural: **GB200 120kW → Feynman ~1MW per rack** — at 1MW, conventional delivery cannot work, and VPD moves from advantage to requirement (the NVDA roadmap carries this as a stated dependency).
