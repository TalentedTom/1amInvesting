## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics (CPO — ELSFP for Broadcom)

## The 1-to-32 Multiplier Effect

This is the explosive growth mechanism:
- Traditional pluggable: 1 transceiver per port
- CPO: 1 switch ASIC → 8 optical engines → 32 ELSFP external laser modules
- **Each CPO system deployed = 32x PCL's ELSFP revenue**
- Broadcom TH6 Davisson (102.4T): laser count increases further

PCL is Broadcom's **exclusive ELSFP supplier**. 4 years of co-development. Chairman: "exclusive supplier for multiple high-end products, direct supply."

## Broadcom CPO Architecture

- TH5 Bailly: 51.2T, world's first CPO entering mass production 2026
- TH6 Davisson: 102.4T CPO, next generation
- CPO saves 65% power vs pluggable, 35% vs LPO
- CPO year-failure rate improved 5x vs traditional transceivers
- FEC: zero errors in 1M hour / 1M dice testing
- Target: <1 pJ/bit (vs 4 pJ/bit for 1.6T pluggable)

## Why External Lasers (ELSFP) Are Required

- Modulators are temperature-sensitive
- Laser inside package = heat interferes with modulation
- External laser = better thermal management + field-serviceable
- This is physics-required architecture, not design choice
- ELSFP is the OIF-standardized form factor (OIF-ELSFP-01.0)
