## Supercycle: DB1 Photonics/CPO — Broadcom's ELSFP Assembly Partner

## Core Thesis: The Module Assembler, Not the Component Maker

PCL is Broadcom's primary partner for **ELSFP (External Laser Source For Pluggable) module assembly** — the module that externalizes the laser from the CPO switch package for thermal management. In CPO architecture the laser cannot live inside the package (too much heat), so it must be externalized into an ELSFP, and **each 51.2T CPO switch needs ~32 ELSFP modules.** That "1 switch becomes 32 modules" multiplier is the revenue-leverage engine: when Broadcom's CPO switch volume scales, PCL's unit demand scales 32x faster.

The structural parallel the thesis rests on: PCL is to the CPO era what a module-assembler like AAOI was to the pluggable era — a small Taiwanese company assembling the *complete module* rather than making a single component, co-located with its anchor customer (factory next door to Broadcom in Penang), supply-qualified through a 10,000-hour reliability program completed in 2025. SIVE makes the laser; PCL puts it into the ELSFP; the ELSFP goes into Broadcom's CPO switch.

### Goldman Dual-Segment Positioning
PCL (4977.TW) is the only book position Goldman Exhibit 21 names in **two** CPO segments: Optical Engine & FAU ($60.99B) and ELS Module ($10.83B) — combined ~$71.8B of addressable CPO TAM. The ELS-module peer set defines the edge: Senko (private, uninvestable), Sumitomo ($29B+ conglomerate, diluted), Innolight (Chinese, NDAA-blocked for US hyperscalers), PCL (~$430M, public, non-Chinese, Broadcom co-located). PCL may be the only publicly investable, non-Chinese, pure-play ELSFP assembler in the chain.
